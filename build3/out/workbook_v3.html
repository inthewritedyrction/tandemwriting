THE READING PLAN · HOW IT WORKS

How the plan works

Volume Two is the spine. You work through the people in story order, the Parish household first, then House Aurem, the twelve Covenant Daughters, the House of Vane and the 39th Trial. Each day pairs a stretch of Volume Two with the Volume One pages that stretch actually cites, so the scripture and the world are open beside the character they belong to.

Volume One pages marked CITED are the ones a Volume Two page sends you to by number. Everything else in Volume One runs alongside as a steady thread in book order, starting with Bible 101 and the sixty-six books, so the scripture study is spread across the whole plan instead of piled at the front. Both volumes finish on the same day. A day is 10 pages, counting the writing pages. Some days run a little over because a dossier cites a long section, and the next day runs light to make up for it. Pages are counted from the full books, which match the split print files.

Recommended pace

10 pages a day, five days a week, is about 19 weeks. Keep the other two days for the manuscript, or for catching up. The plan is 94 sessions in all, Volume One's 598 pages and Volume Two's 385.

The paces
Light · 6 pages a day · about 33 weeks
Recommended · 10 pages a day · about 19 weeks
Intensive · 15 pages a day · about 14 weeks
Changing pace
Doing two rows in a sitting is the intensive pace. Splitting a row over two sittings is the light one. The order still holds.
