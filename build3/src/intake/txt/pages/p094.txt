           BALAAM      AND   THE    DONKEY            ·   22–24


           A foreign diviner is hired by Balak of Moab to curse Israel. His donkey sees the divine messenger before he does

           villain; the Balaam of chapters 22–24 and the Balaam of later reference behave very dierently, and the shift is a
