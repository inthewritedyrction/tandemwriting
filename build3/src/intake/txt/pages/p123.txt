 GENRE
                                  Wisdom literature turned adversarial — it uses the forms of instruction to interrogate
                                  instruction. Royal testament, observation report, proverb collection, and poem sit side
                                  by side. [C]
 STRUCTURE
                                  No tidy outline, and proposals for one disagree. What is visible: the royal experiment
                                  (1–2), the times poem (3), a run of observations on injustice, labor, wealth, and death
                                  (4–10), advice for the young and the closing poem on age (11–12), then the epilogue.
                                  [C]
