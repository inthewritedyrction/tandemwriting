  THE    SKIN       CHAPTERS                                                                                                            T KC : D O M


  Chapters 13–14 give the priest a diagnostic protocol for a scaling skin condition — inspect, isolate, re-inspect after
  seven days, declare. The word is not modern leprosy; it also applies to fabric and to houses. The priest functions as
  an inspector with the power to exclude and to readmit, and the readmission rite is as detailed as the exclusion. [R]
