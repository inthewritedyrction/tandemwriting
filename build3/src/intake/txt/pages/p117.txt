                                Nehemiah confronts them publicly, calls it wrong, orders restoration of elds, houses,

 THE LIMIT
                                Chapter 5 records immediate relief — elds, houses, and interest restored, secured by
                                oath and personal authority. The later covenant in chapter 10 is where a seventh-year
                                release is formalized as a standing obligation. Neither is a structural change to the
                                lending system or to the imperial tax that drove the borrowing, and the text does not
                                report how long either reform was enforced. [P]

  THE      TA X   IS   THE   ENGINE


  The people say explicitly that they borrowed to pay the king’s tax. The debt crisis is downstream of imperial
  extraction, and the reform touches the local creditor rather than the empire — because the empire is not
  available to be reformed. For an author writing a Dominion economy, that is the whole shape of it. [P]
