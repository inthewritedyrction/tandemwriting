  THE      TEST       THE   T W E LV E   A C T U A L LY   OFFERS


  Micah names prophets who declare peace as long as they are being fed, and trouble for anyone who stops
  feeding them. Read as an application, that yields a functional test rather than a moral one: follow the money
  and see whose interest the message serves. It works on the Sanctum, and on anyone your characters are inclined
  to trust — but it is an interpretive use of Micah, not something the text prescribes. [P]
