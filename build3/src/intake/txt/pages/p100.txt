               HOW        TO   READ   THE       OMISSIONS            H O N E S T LY


               A retelling that assumes its reader already knows the older account is not the same thing as a retelling that hides
               it — and Chronicles cites the older account. “Cover-up” is one available reading. So is: a liturgical history
               written for worship rather than a court chronicle written for judgment. Hold both, and say which one you are
               using. [P]
