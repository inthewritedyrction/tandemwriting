E A R LY   CHRISTIAN    USE      OF   JEWISH     SCRIPTURE


The rst Christians had no New Testament. They had Israel's scriptures, read as testimony about what had just happened to
them — which is a reading strategy, not a neutral inheritance. Every appeal to prophecy in the Gospels is that strategy working in
public.
THE    DEAD    SEA   SCROLLS,         IN   ONE   PA R A G R A P H


A library hidden by a Jewish community and recovered in the twentieth century, containing biblical manuscripts a thousand
years older than the previously standard Hebrew texts. They show a text remarkably stable in most places and genuinely variable
in others — which is the single best correction to the idea that the Hebrew Bible either never changed or was rewritten
wholesale.
