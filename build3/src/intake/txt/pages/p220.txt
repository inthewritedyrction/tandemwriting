A woman married into a family, widowed, married to the next brother by
law, widowed again, and then sent back to her father's house while the
family quietly withholds the third son — leaving her in permanent legal
limbo, neither wife nor free. She has no court to appeal to. So she veils
herself, positions herself on the road her father-in-law will travel, and takes
his seal, his cord and his sta as a pledge. When she is later accused of
immorality and sentenced to burn, she produces the three items. He says
aloud that she is more righteous than he is, and the text agrees. Her son
enters the royal line.
