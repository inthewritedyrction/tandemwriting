 GENRE
                                  Ritual instruction, addressed mostly to priests and sometimes to the whole assembly. It
                                  is a procedures manual, not a narrative and not a devotional. Read it the way you would
                                  read a professional standard. [C]
 A WA R N I N G
                                  More people quote this book than have read it. Three verses are famous and twenty-
                                  seven chapters are not. Any argument built from Leviticus that cannot say what
                                  chapter 25 requires is quoting selectively. [P]
