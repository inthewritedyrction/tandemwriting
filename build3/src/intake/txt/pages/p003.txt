Work in one of two modes. In study mode you are simply learning, reading an

Discoveries are the actual product. If an entry teaches you that a passage

as a warning is the only person in her chapter who tells the truth, that is not
trivia. It is a change to the novel, and it belongs on the discovery log at the
back with a date beside it, so that six months from now you can tell the
