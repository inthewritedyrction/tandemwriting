WHY THEY MATTER
Reading a poem as a statute is the single most common way this book gets
weaponised, and it is almost always deliberate. A curse is not a command. A
lament is not a doctrine. A proverb is a generalisation that its own collection
elsewhere contradicts. Learn to name what you are holding before you decide
what it obliges anyone to do.
