           THE   MEASURED                  TEMPLE         ·       40–48


           Nine chapters of speci cation: gates, courts, chambers, altar, priestly duties, festival calendar, a river running out of
           the sanctuary, and a redivided land. A priest in exile responds to catastrophe with a building plan and a rota.
