An enslaved foreign woman handed to her mistress's husband as a
reproductive instrument, with no recorded consent and no line of dialogue
until she is already pregnant and being mistreated. She runs. In the desert

child are expelled with bread and a skin of water, and she puts him under a
bush because she cannot watch him die. And she is the one who names God:
the God who sees. Nobody in Genesis, not Abraham, not Sarah, does that.
