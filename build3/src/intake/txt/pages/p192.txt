                        WHY      AN    EMPIRE       CANNOT          C I R C U L AT E   THIS   CHAPTER


                        A permanent land market and inheritable debt are the two mechanisms by which a household is captured for
                        generations. Chapter 25 forbids both among kin, and grounds the ban in the deliverance the Empire also
                        claims. It cannot be denied and it cannot be taught, so it is left in the closed catechism. [P]
