THE ONES THEY ARGUED ABOUT
The tempting theory is that the contested books were the ones hardest to preach from. It is too tidy — the recorded
objections are usually about authorship, provenance, or speci c content, and each book has its own reason. Read the right-
hand column as the reasons actually given, not as a single pattern.
