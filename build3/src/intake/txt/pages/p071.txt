At the height of the state, then all the way down — the book of the temple's
construction and its destruction, written from captivity by people
explaining how they lost everything.
W H AT    H APPE NS


Solomon builds the temple with forced labour, and at his death the
kingdom splits over that grievance. Then a catalogue of kings, each rated in a
sentence, punctuated by prophets who confront them — Elijah against a
royal couple who seize a vineyard by judicial murder. A forgotten scroll is
found and triggers a purge. Then Assyria, Babylon, and the temple burned.
