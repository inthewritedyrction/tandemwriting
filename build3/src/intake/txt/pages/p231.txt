THE MEN OF THE NEW TESTAMENT
Failure, on the record, in the founding documents

Note the pattern across all of these: the men closest to the centre are shown getting it wrong repeatedly, and the
record was kept by the movement they went on to lead. An institution that publishes its founders' failures is
doing something structurally dierent from one that publishes its founders' credentials.
