 STRUCTURE
                                 Chapters 1–15, oppression and departure. Chapters 16–18, the wilderness and the rst
                                 administration. Chapters 19–24, Sinai, the Decalogue, and the covenant code.
                                 Chapters 25–40, the sanctuary instructions, interrupted by the calf. The escape is a
                                 third of the book. [C]
 GENRES INSIDE
                                 Narrative, genealogy, plague cycle, victory poetry, case law, apodictic command, ritual
                                 calendar, and construction speci cation. The tabernacle chapters are an architectural
                                 document. Do not read them the way you read chapter 14. [C]
 NEIGHBORS
                                 Ancient Near Eastern law collections, the Laws of Hammurabi among them, cover
                                 much of the same legal ground in the same case form. Reading the covenant code
                                 beside them shows what is conventional in it and what is not. [R]
