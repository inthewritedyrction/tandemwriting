  ON    THE    PHRASE         THIS   C O M PA N I O N   WILL    NOT   USE


  “Ethnic cleansing with a liturgy” imports a twentieth-century legal category into a text whose scale, mechanism,
  and de nition of the group are all disputed. It also attens a real and serious ethical problem into a slogan,
  which makes it easier to dismiss. The ethical dispute is preserved on this page in full; the phrase is not used, and
  no formulation of the objection is treated as accurate simply because it is forceful. [P]
