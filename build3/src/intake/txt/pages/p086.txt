W H ER E    WE      A R E


On a prison island, writing to seven congregations under pressure from an

empire that calls its emperor divine. This is banned literature, written in

code, by a man who has been removed for what he said.

W H AT    H A PPE NS


Seven letters assessing seven churches, several of them harshly. Then visions:

a slaughtered lamb on the throne, seals and trumpets and bowls, a beast

whose authority comes from a dragon, a mark required for buying and

selling, and a gorgeously dressed city called a whore whose fall is mourned by

merchants because their trade is ruined. It ends with the city replaced by a

new one where the gates never close.

merchants, who are the ones who weep
