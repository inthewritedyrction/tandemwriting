                 THE    HARDENING             PROBLEM           —   DO   NOT    R E S O LV E   IT   ON   THE   PA G E


                 The text distributes the hardening: sometimes Pharaoh hardens his own heart, sometimes his heart is simply
                 strong, and sometimes God hardens it. The Hebrew uses more than one verb, and the pattern shifts as the cycle
                 proceeds. Whether this is one theology, two sources joined, or a deliberate escalation is argued in the
                 commentaries and not settled here. Any sermon that has never noticed the problem is not reading the book. [R]
