READING THE PAGE
The layout of this companion carries meaning, and once you have learned the ve signals below you
will be able to tell at a glance whether you are reading the text, reading the Empire, reading an
argument that has no settled answer, or looking at space that belongs to you. The signals hold in black
and white, because most of this book will be photocopied at some point in its life.
