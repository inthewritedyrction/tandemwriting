             WHY      A   NAME    IS   THE       THING   HE   ASKS      FOR


             In a world of named local gods, a name is jurisdiction: it tells you whose authority you are invoking and where it
             applies. Moses is not asking for metaphysics; he is asking what to say when the elders ask who sent him.
             Dominion understands this perfectly, which is why the Sanctum controls the forms of address. [P]
