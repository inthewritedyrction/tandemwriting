THE FOUNDERS
Abraham · Jacob · Joseph · Moses — and what the household costs

Read the men of Genesis and Exodus for one thing above all: the founders of the faith are not presented as moral
exemplars. They are presented, with startling candour, as men whose obedience repeatedly endangers the people
in their care. An empire that calls itself their heir has to decide whether to keep that candour.

                        Dominion: obedience without question, illustrated by the mountain. The bargaining over the
                        city is never preached, because it teaches that God can be argued with.

                        renamed after wrestling all night with something he cannot beat, and he limps afterwards.
                        For TKC: the favouritism engine. A house where love is unequally distributed and the children
                        inherit the grudge — your candidate system in miniature.
