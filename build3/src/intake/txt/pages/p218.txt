stops being a reference and starts being a person.

   A   NOTE      ON    THE   UNNAMED   ⌖

   A great many women in this collection are given no name — Jephthah's daughter, Lot's wife, the woman of
   Judges 19, the bleeding woman, the Samaritan at the well. The namelessness is itself information: about who
