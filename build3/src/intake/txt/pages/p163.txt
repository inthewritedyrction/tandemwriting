III          ·   DOMINION       AS   LICENSE


A grant of stewardship over the earth converted into a warrant for extraction, conquest, and the taking of
inhabited land. The verse concerns creatures and soil; it says nothing about authority over other peoples.

IV           ·   CHILDBEARING         AS    SENTENCE


Pain in childbirth read as a penalty properly borne, and used to refuse women relief, medical and otherwise. A
line describing loss, enforced as a divine requirement.
