  HOW      TO    READ      IT   WITHOUT      LY I N G   IN   EITHER      DIRECTION


  This code is neither an abolition document nor an endorsement of chattel slavery as later practiced. It regulates a
  debt-bondage system common to its world, imposes limits some neighboring collections do not, and leaves enslaved
  women with markedly fewer exits than men. Say all three. Do not print “the Bible abolished slavery” and do not
  print “the Bible authorized the plantation.” [P]
