THE WOMEN WHO RAN THINGS
Named, titled, and working — in the same letters used to silence women

One fact worth writing on the inside of your skull: the letters containing the silencing verses also greet a long list
of women by name, with job titles. Any institution has to choose which list is the rule.

PHOEBE                     Called a deacon of her congregation and a patron — a word for someone who funds and

                           answered questions about it. Most translations quietly render her title servant.

Romans 16                  printed as a man's, on no manuscript evidence, because commentators could not accept a
