  THE    CENTRAL        T KC    FINDING


  The Empire’s own scripture contains a clause forbidding the Emperor to accumulate wealth, weapons, and
  women, and requiring him to copy out the law by hand. Dominion cannot delete it. It must reinterpret it —
  most plausibly by declaring the Emperor exempt as the law’s author rather than its subject. That single move is
  the theological center of the Empire, and it is available to any character who can read. [P]
