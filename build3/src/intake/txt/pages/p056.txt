    THE   WORD     RENDERED     "SUBMIT"


    It is voluntary in form rather than commanded from outside — the construction implies something the subject does, not
    something done to her. That is a real feature of the grammar and it is not a solution: a voluntary act performed inside a legal
    system where the alternative is destitution is voluntary in a limited sense. Dominion understands this perfectly. The Empire
    never forces; it makes the alternative unlivable.
