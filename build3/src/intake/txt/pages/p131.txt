 STRUCTURE
                                    Chapters 1–24, judgment on Jerusalem before the fall. 25–32, oracles against
                                    surrounding nations. 33–48, after the fall: restoration, the shepherds, the bones, and a
                                    measured temple. The fall of the city is the hinge, and the book is deliberately built
                                    around it. [C]
 D AT I N G S Y S T E M
                                    The book dates its oracles by elapsed years counted from King Jehoiachin’s deportation
                                    — not by Babylonian regnal years and not from the fall of the city. [C] A community
                                    that les its documents from the year its king was taken, rather than from the reign of
                                    the power that took him, is making a choice about whose time it lives in. Reading that
                                    as deliberate is interpretation. [P]

  DO     NOT    REDUCE          THIS   BOOK      TO   A   MOBILE        GOD
