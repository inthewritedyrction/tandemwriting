 STRUCTURE
                                  1–4 a retelling of the wilderness. 5–11 the commandments, the Shema, and the sermon                             T KC : D O M



                                  on loyalty. 12–26 the law code proper. 27–28 blessings and curses. 29–30 covenant
                                  renewal. 31–34 succession, a song, a blessing, a grave. [C]
 GENRE
                                  Sermon around a law code, in a shape that resembles ancient Near Eastern treaties
                                  between a great king and a subject people: history, terms, witnesses, blessings, curses.
                                  Read it as a founding document with a preacher’s voice. [R]
 A WA R N I N G
                                  This book contains the most quoted verse about love of God and the most brutal
                                  commands about conquest, in the same speech, from the same mouth. Any
                                  presentation that gives you one and not the other is selling you something. [P]
