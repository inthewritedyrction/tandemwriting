  [C] CONFIRMED
                                  The text says this, or the manuscript evidence shows it. You can check it in any study Bible
                                  with a critical apparatus.
  [R] ARGUED
                                  Responsible scholars disagree. The companion prints the dispute and the strongest case
                                  on each side, and does not print a verdict.
  [P] POSSIBLE
                                  A TKC construction — something about Dominion, the Sanctum, or the manuscript,
                                  not a claim about scripture. Test it against the manuscript before use.
  [CANON]
                                  A Possible that has been ruled on and promoted. Fixed for the manuscript until you rule
                                  otherwise.

   ON     THE      SECONDARY          L I T E R AT U R E


   The works below are standard reference points, not an endorsement of any one position. Where two of them
   disagree — and several do, sharply — the companion prints the disagreement rather than picking. Several are
   technical commentaries that assume Hebrew or Greek; you do not need them to use this book. They are listed
   so that any claim in it can be traced.
