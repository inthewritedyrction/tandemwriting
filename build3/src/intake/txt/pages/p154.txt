             THE   JOSEPH          M AT E R I A L   IS   AN   A D M I N I S T R AT I O N      S TO R Y    T KC


             A famine is forecast, grain is centralized, and over the course of the crisis the population sells its livestock, then its
             land, then itself into servitude to the crown in exchange for food. The narrative reports it without visible
             criticism, as competent administration. It is also the mechanism by which a free population becomes a labor
             force, and Exodus opens on the consequence. Whether Genesis intends any judgment on it is a reading the text
             does not settle. [P]
