               THE   READING       THE     PULPIT     SKIPS


               Two of these women are professionals who lie to the state, and the text records approval. Two are mothers
               running an operation across a class line. One is a daughter of the regime. Any account of Exodus that begins
               with a burning bush has skipped the part where ordinary women had already made the deliverer possible. [P]
