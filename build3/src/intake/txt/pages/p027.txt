SIX INSTRUMENTS
The Empire does not need to change a word of the canon. Everything below is documented practice somewhere in
history, and none of it requires editing scripture. Rank them by what Dominion would reach for rst.
I    ·       THE       CALENDAR                                                                                                                        RANK ______


Which feasts exist, which are civic, which are quietly dropped. A calendar decides what a population rehearses annually — and a
holiday nobody keeps is a doctrine nobody remembers.
II       ·       THE   PUBLIC   READINGS                                                                                                               RANK ______


The lectionary. Cheapest and strongest instrument on the page: for most citizens, scripture is what gets read aloud. The rest of the
book is technically available and functionally silent.
III          ·   CLERGY     ACCESS                                                                                                                     RANK ______


Who is trained, in what, and who is permitted to teach it. Control the seminary and you do not have to control the pulpit —
Prelate Huldah's assessment team is this instrument with a person's name on it.
IV           ·   THE    SCHOOLBOOKS                                                                                                                    RANK ______


Which stories children learn as stories, and in which order. The slowest instrument and the only one that compounds — a
generation taught one shape of the book will hear anything else as novelty.

interpretive work in any Bible a citizen actually owns — and none of it is scripture.
VI           ·   C O M M E N TA R Y   &   EMPHASIS                                                                                                     RANK ______


What the Broadcast says a passage means, repeated until the gloss is inseparable from the verse. The only instrument that works
retroactively on people who already own the book.
