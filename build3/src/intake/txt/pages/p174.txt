                     MIRIAM,      PROPHET       AND    SONG-LEADER


                     This is her introduction by title, and it is the rst time a woman in the Torah is called a prophet. She leads a
                     musical company; the verse she sings is the poem’s own opening. Some scholars have argued that the whole song
                     was originally hers, on the strength of that placement — a proposal worth knowing and not a settled result. [R]
