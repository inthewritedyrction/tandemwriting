CHAPTERS,      VERSES,   AND       HEADINGS                 ARE    NOT    SCRIPTURE


Chapter divisions were added in the thirteenth century, verse numbers in the sixteenth, and section headings by modern editors.
They make a text ndable and quotable — genuinely useful — and they also impose breaks the author did not write. Quoting a
single verse is normal and often ne; the question is whether the unit you quoted is doing the work of the unit that surrounds it.
Meaning may sit in a phrase, a paragraph, a chapter, a whole book, or the genre.
