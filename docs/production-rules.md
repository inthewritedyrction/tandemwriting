# The Tandem Companion — Production Rules

Standing rules for assembling the Companion. They apply to every page of the build and sit alongside the Code Assembly Brief.

## Rule 1 — Writability overrides page compression

*Author ruling, 2026-10-09. Verbatim.*

The approved Direction C visuals establish the Companion's visual system and page logic, not immutable page dimensions.

This is a printed working companion. It must be comfortable to handwrite in.

During assembly:

- Treat the writing space shown in the approved TKC-scale samples as a minimum, not a maximum.
- Never shrink body text, prompts, tables, handwriting lines, or decision fields merely to force content onto one page.
- If a development task needs more room, increase the page count.
- A one-page entry may become two pages; a two-page spread may become three or four pages when needed.
- Major scene work, character development, plot architecture, magic development, relationship development, and open canon decisions may receive a full page or full facing spread of writing space.
- Keep generous writable space at the bottom of pages. Do not let prompts, rules, decision rows, handwriting lines, or tables crowd or collide with the footer.
- Establish a consistent footer safe zone across the book. No working content may enter it.
- Fix clipping, overlap, awkward page breaks, orphaned prompts, cramped tables, and unusably short writing fields during assembly.
- When something does not fit cleanly, flow it forward rather than compress it.
- Preserve Direction C's route strip, hierarchy, status language, source/provenance treatment, typography roles, accent system, and grayscale readability.
- Do not rewrite canon or change statuses in order to solve a layout problem. Layout adapts to content, not the reverse.

The finished 3SL Companion should feel like a real notebook/reference book I can sit down with and write inside for hours, not a PDF optimized to use the fewest pages possible.

Use the approved visual PDF as the design reference and apply these production rules throughout the entire build.

## How the build applies Rule 1

The measurements below come from the approved Visual Directions pages. They are floors: the build may go larger, never smaller.

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trim | US Letter, 8.5 × 11 in (816 × 1056 px at 96 dpi) |
| Side margins | 0.75 in (72 px), as approved |
| Handwriting line pitch | 30 px (0.3125 in, about 7.9 mm); never tighter |
| Body / prompt type | Sizes as approved (16 px body, 15 px prompts, 12 px mono labels); never reduced to fit |
| Footer safe zone | Bottom 1.0 in (96 px) of every page holds only the footer rule, footer text and section tab. Working content ends at or above that line. |
| Footer text | Breadcrumb, MS CHECK and folio each stay on one line. If they don't fit, the footer takes a second row inside the safe zone. Type never shrinks. |
| Writing field floor | At least 4 handwriting lines under any open prompt. Major development prompts (scene, character, plot architecture, magic, relationship, open canon decisions) may take a full page or facing spread. |
| Tables | Writable rows keep their approved height; a table that runs long continues on the next page with its header row repeated. |
| Decision rows | SAFE TO DEFER / DECIDE BY / DECIDED / LOG / DATE always stays with its prompt; if it can't fit above the safe zone, the prompt and its row flow forward together. |
| Page counters | "N OF M" kickers, "WORK ON" references, spread labels and cross-references are regenerated after flowing. They are layout data, not canon. |
