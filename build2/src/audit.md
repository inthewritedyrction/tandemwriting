## Method

Every Word file was read from extracted paragraph and table content. Every workbook was read sheet-by-sheet, including formulas and cached values. Classification is based on content, not filename or modification date. “Current” means usable now within its stated domain; it does not mean globally controlling. “Supporting” means informative but subordinate to manuscript or ruling. “Legacy” means a superseded story generation. “Needs Author Ruling” means two or more plausible generations remain live. TKC files are isolated from Tandem canon.

## Architecture Handoff — Audit Gate Passed

On 2026-09-26, the author directed that questions answerable during writing must not delay construction. The inventory, claim-level source map, high-impact conflict register, and TCD-001–064 rulings are sufficient to begin Companion Architecture. The proposed TCD-065–074 multiple-choice questions were **not answered or adopted** and do not become canon. No new decision IDs are assigned merely by asking questions.

Build architecture in this order: (1) an authority/status key distinguishing ruled canon, current drafted text, supported provisional material, legacy, and open development; (2) the shared series spine for established geography, chronology, magic ancestry, boundary/prophecy architecture, and knowledge limits; (3) four book dossiers with current protagonist, central engine, ruled outcome constraints, and explicit open slots; (4) cross-book continuity matrices and an author-ruling queue used only when a specific page would otherwise assert an unresolved fact. These are structural headings and information slots, **not Companion front matter or drafted canon prose**.

Proceed using established claims only. An unruled plan may guide a chapter or dossier as a labeled proposal, but no synopsis, timeline, or lore entry may present its disputed ending/identity/causation as settled. Ask for a ruling at the point of actual contradiction or publication-level commitment; otherwise leave a clearly marked open slot and keep building. The Cael/Caelen tablet, First Shellward's pre-Drowning Anchor terminology, and the blueprint's detailed Book One endpoint are drafting flags, not gates on architecture. Architecture is ready; a finished, unqualified canon encyclopedia is not.

## Preliminary Domain-Based Source Authority Map

1. **Explicit author rulings and dated Canon Decision Log entries** control every domain they address.
2. **Current manuscript pages** control what has happened on-page: POV, scene chronology, revealed identity, performed magic, relationships, and immediate consequences.
3. **Book-specific locked constraints** control the intended premise and still-undrafted core engine unless a manuscript page or later author ruling supersedes them.
4. **Book-specific current worldbuilding/character files** control geography, institutions, culture, baseline magic, and backstory only where the manuscript is silent and the material does not conflict with a ruling.
5. **Current outlines/chapter plans** control undrafted plot only after their generation is confirmed. They never retroactively overwrite drafted scenes without an author ruling.
6. **Supporting artifacts**—timelines, placement tables, genealogy, spreadsheets, quote maps—control only their narrow domain after consistency checks.
7. **Marketing files** can describe positioning but cannot establish canon.
8. **Legacy drafts and plans** are evidence of creative history only. They cannot fill canon gaps.
9. **TKC files** are structural/philosophical models only and have zero Tandem authority.

Shared-domain guardrails established by TCD-015–064:

- **Objective geography outranks cultural cosmology.** Remnia, Somnoria, Thalara, and the Underside occupy one physical world. “World” and “realm” may remain valid in-world language for isolated cultural perspectives, but not literal parallel-universe canon.
- **Knowledge is actor- and time-specific.** No source may promote one character’s, institution’s, class’s, or nation’s knowledge into universal knowledge. Existence, location, function, route, history, and full Tandem knowledge are separate states.
- **Routes are physical-magical transit.** They use real passages and may magically cross barriers, distance, or concealed geography; they are not dimensional portals. A route’s scale and capability must be individually established.
- **Artifact truth outranks local interpretation.** The Oneiros Prism is a fragmented cross-land structural artifact. Somnorian dream-network uses and beliefs are accurate only within their observed scope unless independently confirmed as objective history.
- **Threat layers stay distinct.** Nightmare the criminal organization and Nightmare the ancient entity are both real. Public, operational, inner-circle, and objective knowledge must not be collapsed.
- **Institutional authority is domain-specific.** The High Council governs Somnoria nationally; the four Houses exercise representation and political power within that structure; the Dream Master’s Guild regulates professional dream practice. Overlapping jurisdictions remain open.
- **Mixed legacy master plans require claim-level review.** `Full 4 Series Flesh Out` is a recovery pool, never a blanket canon source. Its poem architecture survives; every other shared or book-level claim must be separately classified.
- **Maps control precise placement only after archive review.** TCD-025–027 establish the macro arrangement and known crossings. Danyelle’s existing Remnia map will control precise provincial coordinates once added; remembered placement is not a substitute for reviewing it.
- **Shared terminology does not establish shared allegiance.** Nightmare magic, the Nightmare Abyss, and the Nightmare organization are distinct unless a ruling explicitly connects them. Caelum’s nightmare affinity does not make him an organizational member.
- **Relationship magic must be mechanically earned.** Reverie and Cassius can touch and separate safely. The known danger begins with active magical synchronization and concentrates in Cassius’s Labyrinth scar, not compulsory physical proximity.
- **Political titles follow canon rules, not legacy labels.** “Conqueror” belongs to the decisive surviving Piece, not automatically the King. In the current forfeit outcome, Katherine is Conqueror because her sanctioned climax with Melena creates the decisive state.
- **Shared ancestry does not erase mechanical difference.** All four modern magical traditions derive from one primordial substrate, but each is now a genuinely distinct system with its own access, costs, classifications, and cultural history. Similar principles do not make abilities interchangeable.
- **Institutional labels are not objective magic taxonomy.** “Crossblood” is a scholarly, political, historical, or institutional explanation for suspected mixed inheritance. It cannot serve as a universal biological category or catch-all for unusual magic without case-level proof.
- **Boundary components need not be symmetrical.** The Prism and Leviathan participate in the same larger boundary-maintenance problem/system, but they are not equivalent components and do not establish four matching anchors.
- **The Prism restricts Nightmare without containing it.** Nightmare predates the present boundary architecture and exists beyond or behind a boundary reinforced in part by the Prism. “Cage” may survive as limited Somnorian interpretation, not literal artifact anatomy.
- **Repeated nouns do not establish shared artifacts.** Tavian’s “fragment” is a Thalaran memory-shell relic, not automatically a Drowned Canticle passage or Prism fragment. Its information layers require separate provenance.
- **Biological, legal, public, and political lineage are separate.** Tobias is Katherine and Melena’s shared biological father; that does not independently settle Katherine’s records, legitimacy, title, succession rights, or public knowledge.
- **Remnian power is overlapping, not singular.** Crown, provincial leaders, Leadership Board, Elemental Court, Sanctum, and military authorities possess distinct and sometimes competing jurisdictions. Public hierarchy cannot substitute for an actual-power map.
- **The FTE forfeit is an act of agency.** Katherine creates the decisive state by saving/stabilizing Melena; Melena chooses survival over continued sacrifice. Tiran can support or operationalize that choice, but formal surrender authority must follow the Tournament rules.
- **Operational language does not erase character agency.** Caelum’s “asset” framing describes how he thinks, not Melena’s objective status. Their relationship is a transactional alliance with coercive leverage on both sides.
- **Magical dependency is not romantic consent.** Silas and Isola cross into explicit mutual romance during 3SL, but the tether Silas created without her consent cannot prove or substitute for voluntary intimacy.
- **The Divide is rupture plus response.** A non-deliberate natural, celestial, cosmic, or magical rupture destabilized the primordial system; ancient peoples then created, adapted, bound, activated, or repurposed containment and boundary structures. That response hardened the crisis into long-term separation but did not cause the initiating rupture.
- **Convergence is a transition state, not a promised outcome.** It is the progressive destabilization and re-entanglement of lands, magical branches, boundary systems, routes, artifacts, and suppressed history. It does not inherently mean salvation, apocalypse, physical continental merger, or homogenized magic.
- **Prophecy reconstruction is not artifact assembly.** No intact authoritative Shattered Veil master is presently known in-world. The four national texts are divergent historical descendants, and recovering the original truth requires comparison plus external evidence rather than joining physical text fragments.
- **Shared ritual inheritance is not temporal causation.** Selah and Katherine independently perform the same inherited Elders’ Prayer about two years apart. Selah’s individual performance leaves no specific echo, reservoir, route transmission, or power that Katherine accesses.
- **Institutional phenotype labels do not settle magical anatomy.** Katherine truly manifests Frost and Fire, but this is ancestral reversion to less-specialized Remnian elemental inheritance rather than the same objective Fire/Frost Dyad structure as Zephyr. “Dyad” is the Sanctum’s incomplete current label.
- **Major identity reveals require layered evidence.** Melena’s investigation, confrontation, verification, and Michelle’s personal testimony are distinct stages in the Katherine–Melena sister reveal. No single magical reaction, file, or exposition dump can carry the whole truth.
- **Atlas’s injury begins in the Conduit Trial.** Force-synchronizing his dense/control Frost with Katherine’s reactive Frost creates a progressive inward overcorrection injury. It is not a pre-existing ordinary accumulation of Element Rot or an invented prior corruption history.
- **Narrative agency and formal authority remain separate.** Melena chooses survival, Tiran advises and operationalizes, and the living, conscious King Maxim formally authorizes the provincial forfeit. The Sanctum accepts the outcome but does not compel its original decision.
- **Nightmare Awareness is not Nightmare influence.** At ADST Book One’s end, the ancient entity becomes aware and the House Aether Prism fragment becomes perceptible to it. The entity does not yet independently communicate, influence, manifest through the Network, run an operation, or cross into waking reality.
- **Series chronology uses rolling handoffs.** TSWK and the next Shadowmere book begin directly after FTE/ADST, allowing only days or small weeks of logistics, and substantially overlap one another. Plot conditions may activate before page one, so background activation, narrative opening, climax, and resolution require separate dates.
- **The Drowning is later than the Divide.** It is Thalara’s later regional catastrophe. The First Seal is a distinct emergency response that bound the preexisting Leviathan, made an older Seal Song operational, and sacrificed part of Thalara; deterioration of Divide-era systems as a cause is not established.
- **Convergence has a threshold, not a single origin event.** Long boundary degradation produces latent localized symptoms by FTE/ADST. Late ADST’s Prism/Network crisis is the first established threshold from mostly local failures to cross-system responsiveness; Nightmare’s Awareness is an indicator/consequence, not the cause. Other anomalies need case-level proof.
- **The Elders’ Prayer restores a viable living magical pattern.** It can stabilize a dying person and secondary bodily functions when magical disruption is causal, but cannot resurrect completed death or regenerate destroyed anatomy. Selah and Katherine’s different expressions share that objective function.
- **Katherine’s Fire is an alternate expression, not a sealed ordinary second channel.** Her less-specialized ancestral capacity developed a dominant Frost configuration; ordinary extreme stress did not unlock Fire. Catastrophic loss/severance of the Frost configuration permits rerouting into Fire. No deliberate magical suppression is established.
- **Concealment has different owners by layer.** Lucia/the Crown originated Katherine’s public/legal erasure; the Sanctum enforced secrecy and controlled records; Tobias cooperated in non-acknowledgment; Michelle raised/protected Katherine within those constraints. Motives, consents, exact records, and initial public exposure remain open.
- **Nightmare organization operates; the ancient entity does not.** The organization targets Reverie and attempts to rupture the Dream Network using Thorne’s research, alignment, and living conduit/anchor configuration to breach a deeper boundary. Reverie and Cassius stop that breach, the Network survives, and the entity gains no usable channel.
