The public hears “support for Elias after his mother’s death.” The royal houses hear “a legal way to stand over him without calling it custody.” The Hastings elders take heavy seats to keep the Sanctum from stacking the boards. The Lockwoods are given thin representation on purpose. It’s the first official attempt to normalize Sanctum proximity to the heir line, and everybody understands it’s not temporary.
## February 14: Sebastian’s Reputation Stops Being “Minho’s Brother”
Prince Sebastian (19) steps into the year as his own name finally, not just the third Augustine who followed Minho. He’s in Year Nine upper-level military courses with Katherine, and people start clocking that his strength isn’t just physical — he’s a strategist with a terrifying read on people. This is when command starts pairing him with pieces who think like him, not because they’re friends but because the army can smell a future tactician.
## February 23: Cordelia Is Publicly Resettled With Her Lockwood Grandparents
Princess Cordelia (15) is formally moved into her grandparents’ household under Aracelia’s guardianship decree, and the palace frames it as healing. The real purpose is protection. Aracelia wants Cordelia out from under daily Sanctum eyes and inside a Lockwood home that can’t be restructured by advisory committees without making the Sanctum look like they’re kidnapping a grieving child. The move goes through quickly enough that the Sanctum can’t build a counter-narrative in time, and that speed matters.
## March 7: The Empire’s Mourning Seal Lifts, and Politics Get Loud
The final memorial for Lucia is staged like a national courtroom. Aracelia makes her tribute about Lucia’s leadership, her reforms, and her protection of royal youth. The Sanctum counters within days by shifting the public story toward “Lucia’s sacrifice ensured order.” The grief becomes a political battlefield. Whoever owns the memory gets to shape the future, and the Sanctum does not intend to lose.
## March 5: Zephyr Quietly Arrives in Old Prosperity’s Shadow
Prince Zephyr (19) is already in Old Prosperity by now, but this is the month he becomes visible to the right people. He isn’t climbing like Minho and Sebastian — he’s moving sideways, embedding himself in the training circuits, dueling halls, and social corners where information travels faster than rank. Because Zephyr doesn’t need a title to be dangerous. He needs proximity. The boys who underestimate him this month are the ones who won’t survive him later.
## March 18: The “Heir Readiness” Narrative Begins
Court circles start whispering that Elias (21) is “too young to shoulder a fractured empire alone.” Nobody serious believes he’s unready. The goal is to make the public comfortable with oversight, so that Sanctum handlers at his elbow look like support instead of a power grab. Aracelia hears the narrative forming and begins building a public counter-image of Elias as composed, capable, and loved.
## April 1: The Sanctum Attempts a Travel Audit on Melena
The Sanctum launches a formal audit into Melena’s “provincial visits,” leaning on paperwork and safety clauses to justify why a nineteen-year-old princess has been off-grid so often. Lorelei and Helena stonewall in public, and Aracelia backs them from the throne, because they all understand what this is really about: the Sanctum trying to stop Melena from finding whatever Lucia buried in Shadowmere. Melena doesn’t pause her trips. She just gets better at disappearing.
## April 9: Sebastian Officially Enlists in Old Prosperity
Prince Sebastian (19) formally joins the Armed Forces in Old Prosperity, following Minho’s path and relinquishing his succession placement. London Town frames it as honor and healing through service. Old Prosperity treats it as what it is: the Augustine family moving their pieces to safety before the Sanctum decides where they belong. Sebastian doesn’t say much publicly, but everyone who saw his 2093 visit knows he left because he understood the palace isn’t safe anymore.
## April 19: Melena’s Shadowmere Network Expands Into a Real Pipeline
Shadowmere stops being a place Melena sneaks to and becomes a place that feeds her. The people Lucia trusted begin treating Melena like successor, not child. She starts receiving documents without asking, warnings before danger hits, and names that don’t exist on any capital ledger. Every trip back and forth this month hardens her. By now, she’s not searching randomly — she’s pulling a thread Lucia already knotted for her.
## April 21: Minho Receives a Commendation for Bravery
Prince Minho (20) receives a commendation from Old Prosperity command for a classified operation. The capital press keeps it vague, but Old Prosperity celebrates him like one of their own. Every commendation pushes Minho toward the kind of rank that can shelter his brothers from any Sanctum attempt to drag them back. He’s not just building a career. He’s building a bunker.
## May 14: Louis Turns 14 Under a Watchful Empire
Prince Louis (now 14) hits his birthday in a palace that still doesn’t feel like home. His celebration is small, family-only, mostly inside the Lockwood household. The Sanctum tries to insert ceremonial oversight “for tradition,” and Aracelia blocks it by making the celebration private. Louis is old enough now to understand what’s happening, and young enough for it to terrify him. This birthday becomes one of the first moments he realizes that safety in London Town is something you negotiate, not something you’re given.
## May 23: Katherine’s Name Becomes Loud in the Province
Katherine (19) is no longer just “that talented Frost girl.” She’s now the one everybody expects to see on the Old Prosperity roster when Selection comes. She’s been stacking performance, field-tests, and duels since she got here, and she moves like someone who understands war as a language. The Sanctum’s media blockade makes her harder to track, which only adds to her myth. Old Prosperity loves a ghost who can fight.
## June 2: London Town Begins Quiet Delegation Strategy Meetings
With London Town eligible again in 2096, the capital starts its Delegation strategy behind closed doors. The Sanctum wants spectacle and control; Aracelia wants her family alive. These meetings are tense because Lucia used to be the one who could stare the Sanctum down without blinking. Now Aracelia is doing it while grieving, and everybody knows the Sanctum will test her limits this year.
## June 18: Elias Is Pushed Into “Visibility Tours”
Elias (21) is scheduled for a string of carefully staged public appearances across London Town and two allied provinces. The Sanctum sells it as heir-confidence rebuilding. Elias feels what it really is: a leash made of applause. Aracelia lets the tours happen because the people need to see him steady, but she fights the Sanctum on every detail so they can’t claim him as theirs on camera.
## July 4: Aracelia Publicly Doubles Down on Lucia’s Legacy
Aracelia (57) announces a Lucia Legacy Initiative with Melena as its face. It looks like charity. It’s chess. Aracelia uses Remnia’s love for Lucia as a shield around her daughter: any Sanctum move against Melena now risks looking like disrespect to a national martyr. The Sanctum can’t touch Melena openly, so they start squeezing everything around her instead.
## July 19: The Hastings and Lockwoods Start Competing for Elias’s Orbit
With Stability Councils in place, both houses begin subtle positioning around Elias (21). Hastings relatives push for more joint appearances to reinforce imperial continuity. Lockwoods push for private family time to preserve Elias’s identity outside the crown. Elias feels pulled in both directions, and the Sanctum enjoys that tension because a divided family makes a pliable heir.
## September 2: Cordelia Turns 16 and Isn’t Allowed to Be a Kid
Cordelia (16) reaches her birthday inside her grandparents’ household. The celebration is warm, but smaller than tradition demands because the Sanctum is still monitoring the Lockwood minors as political assets. Cordelia is old enough now to understand the stakes and young enough to hate that understanding. This birthday hardens her. She starts asking questions adults are scared to answer.
## September 9: Melena Turns 19, One Year After Losing Lucia
Melena’s birthday hits like a bruise. The Empire stages a memorial-celebration fusion to honor her as Lucia’s legacy, and Melena stands in it because she has to, not because she wants to. This is the first moment she fully feels how the throne tries to turn grief into obedience. She smiles for Remnia, then goes home and reads Lucia’s letter again like scripture.
## October 3: Elias Turns 22 Under Sanctum Eyes
Elias turns 22, and the palace throws a controlled celebration meant to show the public a steady heir. The Sanctum keeps a tight perimeter, treating his birthday like a security event instead of a family rite. Aracelia makes sure the people see Elias cherished. The Sanctum makes sure Elias feels watched. It’s the same war, just quieter.
## November 11: The Sanctum Attempts to Amend Youth Oversight Law
A proposed Youth Oversight Amendment hits council floors, framed as “standardization after tragedy.” It would give the Sanctum direct authority to override guardian households in “national interest.” Aracelia blocks it publicly and brutally, framing the amendment as disrespect to Lucia’s memory and a danger to every Remnian child. The people side with her. The Sanctum backs off for now, but they don’t forget the humiliation.
## December 6: Old Prosperity Advances Minho’s Command Track
Minho (21) is formally placed on a faster command track within Old Prosperity. It’s the province making a statement: this prince is ours, his loyalty is proven, and his rank will be defended like any other officer’s. Minho understands what that means for 2096. He is almost where he needs to be.
## December 31: The Empire Walks Into 2096 With the Board Already Set
By year’s end, the Sanctum has failed to seize the heirs cleanly, but they have succeeded in tightening the ecosystem around them. Aracelia has protected her grandkids in public, but she knows protection is running on borrowed time. Melena has Shadowmere in her bones now. Elias has learned what captivity looks like when it’s wrapped in ceremony. Old Prosperity has become the Augustine fortress. The empire calls it stability. The people call it grief. The royal children feel it for what it is: a countdown.
# 2096 ER: The Events of For Their Entertainment
## August 16: Selection Is Announced — London Town vs Old Prosperity
Selection drops mid-August exactly as tradition demands. The empire pretends it’s surprised. Nobody actually is. London Town and Old Prosperity have been a loaded gun all year, and this is the Sanctum pulling the trigger while smiling for the broadcast. The capital erupts in performance patriotism. Old Prosperity goes silent and starts sharpening knives.
## November 2096: The Passing of Lord Clyde Hastings
Lord Clyde Hastings, husband to Princess Alexandria Melena Mignonette, Duchess of Alleia, dies after a brief but tightly controlled decline. The Sanctum releases minimal detail, framing the loss as an imperial tragedy and requesting national restraint. Alexandria withdraws from public duties to mourn, and her household closes ranks at Alleia.Her daughters, Princess Amalia Danyelle Hastings (34) and Princess Evangeline Kelly Hastings (33) return to the capital for the mourning period as visible representatives of the Alleian dukedom. Their presence matters: Amalia is already treated in court as a stabilizing heir figure in Alleia, while Evangeline’s sharper political instincts make her a quiet favorite among the imperial strategists. After Lucia’s death, the empire has learned to watch the daughters as carefully as the sons, and Alexandria’s girls are no exception.
