# The Prophecy of the Shattered Veil 
## I. THE FIRST FALL 
Before the stars were taught to burn,Before the tide was told to turn,A voice was born in blood and breath—A crown was forged, a vow to Death.
The Order rose with chains of grace,To bind the world in rightful place.But power kept is power sown,And rot will bloom in gardens grown.
## II. THE SCATTERING
They danced beneath a gilded stage,Their laughter hiding quiet rage.
They begged the flame to make things right,Then fed it truth and turned from light.
They whispered wrongs they dared not speak,And carved their grief into the weak.
They rose with crowns not earned but given—A curse beneath their blood was written.
A dream unspooled across the sky,A mirror cracked, a perfect lie.
A fate once fractured found its thread,But stitched its hope through knots of dread.
These hollowed walls, now wrapped in mold,Remember what they can’t behold.
And from the ashes, sharp and bare—The truth still waits, but never fair.
## III. THE DIVIDE
Four truths were cast to four-born lands,Each gripped by time’s decaying hands.
One held the storm with breath and bone—Their names were laws, their heirs were known. (Remnia)
One broke the night to mine their dreams,And built their gods from shattered schemes. (Somnoria)
One walked where waves devoured the past,With songs too cursed to ever last. (Western nation)
One slept beneath the smoking stone,And taught the dead to die alone. (Underside / buried realm)
Four branches bent by shadow’s thread,Each blind to how the roots had bled.
## IV. THE RETURN
When time unravels spine from soul,When suns go dark and rivers toll,
When every oath is stripped of name,And kin bear crowns they cannot claim—
The veil will split. The mask will fall.No land will thrive. No voice will call.
But in the ruin, cracked and cold,A single page remains to hold.
Not war, nor flame, nor faith will win—But what was lost remembered in.
And those who rise with open eyesWill curse the stars—and still survive.

# The Remnian Chronicles
## Book 1: For Their Entertainment
THEME: Would you kill to save a life?
CORE QUESTION: What if the thing you believed in your whole life was never meant to protect you?
### Premise:
Katherine Nicholas enters the Tournament as the daughter of the legendary Captain Michelle Nicholas, who once won one of the most brutal Tournaments in imperial history. Katherine has been trained her whole life in Old Prosperity — the Empire’s military stronghold — and raised on the Underside version of the prophecy. She believes it to be truth because all Frost in Old Prosperity are taught that version of the poem. 
What she doesn’t realize: Old Prosperity has kept secret cultural ties to the Underside, and cadets have been quietly taught the Mourning Verse for generations.
Melena Lockwood is of imperial blood, a princess by birthright, and this is the year she is of age to enter the Tournament. She complies — but also manipulates Cordelia’s score, swapping it out so that Tiran is entered instead. It’s a political move to protect her sister and preserve London Town’s favor.
The Empire is watching both of them. For very different reasons. And both girls have no idea that the price of survival has already been calculated.
### Tournament Dynamics:
The Tournament is a sanctioned, high-stakes performance where the “Pieces” from two provinces compete every year to determine who will have a chance to sit at the Imperial Board and determine how policies, laws and trade are shaped for an entire year. The Sanctum curates every challenge to test more than skill — they test loyalty, deception, and internal fracture. The goal is not to crown one champion, but to determine who is loyal, dangerous, or worth watching.
### Challenge Two — Empirical History (Poem Focus):
The challenge presents two versions of the ancient poem:
One: The Breathbound Law (Remnia’s official, redacted version)
Two: The Dreamwalker’s Writ (secretly Somnia’s version)
London Town ends up loosing the challenge because of Melena. No one should know where that document comes from but Melena does because of her time in Shadowmere. It's now that she doesnt know The Breathbound Law. Everyone in Remnia does. It's that Melena has recently LEARNED Somnoria's version of the poem in her research. In her healing and pain from the first challenge, Melena accidentally shows that she knows about documents that no one else knows exists. 
### Core Pillars of Book 1:
| Element | Description |
| Melena’s Arc | Plays the game to protect her brother who is trapped in Somnoria. Strategic. Master manipulator. Keeps people right where she wants them until it is time for her to use them. Slowly comes to realize that even she cannot protect everyone. |
| Katherine’s Arc | Believes in honor, legacy, and truth after being raised in Old Prosperity. She believes in what the Empire has told her (because she’s a literal product of the strongest woman in the military). It’s the Tournament that slowly starts to break her perception of reality. |
| Core Relationship | Two girls with different versions of the same bloodline, both shaped by mothers who taught them how to survive a world they no longer control. |
| Theme Deepened | It’s not just “Would you kill to save a life?” It’s “Would you choose the lie to protect someone you love — or die clinging to the truth?” |
| Political Undercurrent | The Empire doesn’t kill rebellion. It catalogs it. The Tournament is surveillance, not sport. |
| Legacy Thread | As children grow, legacy becomes less about what was passed down and more about what they choose to carry. |
### The Poem’s Role in Book 1:
The Breathbound Law becomes a symbol of how history is rewritten to suit those in power.
This moment plants the seed for Book 3 — when Melena begins to realize the poem she loved has been intentionally erased in public records.
### Katherine + Melena = Sisters
Katherine and Melena don’t find out they’re sisters until way deep in the Tournament, but the reveal doesn’t come from some big dramatic moment or a loud scene in the arena. It’s layered, built over time, and neither of them realizes what’s actually happening until it’s too damn late.
Early in the Tournament, Melena is given access to the classified files on all sixteen Old Prosperity Pieces. She gets them by trading intel to the Sanctum in exchange for small favors—things to help London Town stay ahead. She doesn’t go digging through all of them because honestly? She doesn’t see Katherine as a threat. At that point, she’s focused on Atlas, Zephyr, Dax. Katherine’s just a quiet Frost girl with a military legacy and good scores. So Melena skips the file.
What she doesn’t know is that Michelle Nicholas—Katherine’s mother—was slipping her daughter intel too. After stepping down from her position as Captain, Michelle took a behind-the-scenes assignment through the Sanctum. She wasn’t out of the game. She was just moving quieter. And every night, she risked everything to slide Katherine pieces of information. Nothing big enough to flag her, just enough to help her daughter survive. A phrase. A pattern. A faction name. Katherine didn’t even realize it was happening most of the time.
None of it comes to a head until Challenge 6—the Duel. That’s when shit breaks. Melena makes a calculated move and gravely injures Atlas, not out of spite, but strategy. She knows how close Katherine is to him. She knows he’s the one person keeping Katherine grounded. So she takes him out.
Katherine panics. Fully breaks. And instead of freezing up, she performs the Elder’s Prayer—a Frost-only incantation that’s meant to be passed down through legacy. It’s sacred. It can only be used to bring back another Bound Frost. And it works. Atlas comes back from the brink.
That alone is enough to shake people. But then Katherine lashes out. She attacks Melena, not with Frost—but with fire. Her suppression breaks. The grief, the fear, the fury—it all burns through her, literally. And that’s the moment the truth shows up in flames.
After the challenge, Melena finally decides to look at Katherine’s file. And it’s all there. The Order of Silence. The bloodline suppression docs. Her Binding certification. The record of Michelle handing it all over to the Sanctum in exchange for Katherine’s Tournament slot. The signature of Tobias Lockwood, clear as day.
Melena just stares at the folder. She had the truth the whole time and didn’t bother to open it. And now the one girl she underestimated? Is the one girl who might ruin everything.
## Book 2: For Their Atonement
THEME: Survival isn’t redemption.
This book is about the weight that follows you when the fight is over and the world moves on — especially when you’re still carrying the consequences.
CORE QUESTION: “How do you atone for what you became when the world still thinks you played by the rules?”
### WHERE WE’RE STARTING:
Old Prosperity won the Tournament. Officially. Cleanly. The broadcast showed Katherine saving Atlas and ended the coverage right there.
The Dyad reveal? Cut. The fire? Erased.
Only the Sanctum, Melena, and a few key players know what really happened.
Katherine is now stationed in London Town as Minho Augustine’s First Mate in the En Guard.
Atlas is also in London Town — still Captain of the Legion.
And now they’re expected to live like things are normal, when nothing is.
### KATHERINE’S ARC:
The fire is back. The control isn’t.
She’s watching her Binding with Atlas deepen while trying to hide what she’s become.
She’s not trying to expose the Empire. She’s trying to survive it.
And every day that passes, her powers misfire more often — not just because of emotion, but because something bigger is happening beneath the surface.
No one’s saying it out loud, but magic is shifting. Across the board.
### MELENA’S ARC:
She won politically. But the cracks are everywhere.
She didn’t read Katherine’s file until it was too late — and now she’s paying for that choice in silence.
She’s still playing the game, still controlling London Town’s court, but the Sanctum doesn’t trust her like they used to.
And with Katherine living two floors below her, the lie they’ve both been keeping is starting to rot.
### THE EMPIRE’S MOVE:
The Empire and the Sanctum are pretending like nothing is wrong.
But they’ve placed both Katherine and Melena in the same city on purpose.
Not to heal anything — but to watch.
Every mission. Every report. Every slip. The Sanctum is waiting for someone to break.
### THE WORLD IS CHANGING:
This book is the first time Somnoria is named.
Maybe it’s in a diplomatic brief. A training exercise. A coded message. A glitch in a magical report that doesn’t add up.
But it’s there.
And the misfiring magic in Remnia isn’t isolated. It syncs directly with the end of A Dream So Twisted — where Somnoria's dreamscape destabilizes and begins bleeding into reality.
Katherine starts to question whether her Dyad status is actually rare… or just one of many things the Empire has been hiding.
### KEY DYNAMICS:
| Connection | Tension |
| Katherine &amp; Atlas | Their Binding grows more intimate and intense — but she fears what it’s turning her into. |
| Katherine &amp; Minho | Professional. Sharp. Controlled. But Minho sees more than he says, and Katherine doesn’t know what he’s reporting. |
| Melena &amp; Katherine | Not friends. Not enemies. Tethered by a shared lie and a growing list of people who want both of them silenced. |
| The Empire vs Reality | The board may be reset, but the game is unraveling. And people are waking up. |
ENDPOINT:
Katherine’s fire is harder to contain. Her Dyad status isn’t just a secret — it’s a ticking bomb.
Melena starts to realize the Sanctum never planned to protect her either.
The final line isn’t a battle cry. It’s a quiet realization:
“This wasn’t the end of the Tournament. It was the start of something else.”
## Book 3: For Their Resentment
THEME: "If the truth can’t save you, what’s the point of knowing it?"
This book is about the slow unraveling of power. The Empire is fracturing. The players are turning against each other. And the biggest lie of all — the lie of who gets to rule — finally explodes.
CORE QUESTION: “What happens when the legacy you fought to protect no longer includes you?”
### WHERE WE’RE STARTING:
Magic is breaking. Misfires. Dyads forming on accident. Bloodlines revealing things they were never meant to.
Katherine and Melena are both back in London Town. They are barely managing the tension between them, but neither of them can afford to start a war.
Katherine’s fire is getting harder to hide. The Sanctum is getting harder to predict.
And rumors are starting to spread — about the Tournament, about what really happened, about who Katherine might be.
### THE POWER SHIFT:
The Empress dies. Whether by assassination, elemental failure, or illness — doesn’t matter. She’s gone.
And suddenly:
Elias is forced to return.
Katherine is named heir apparent.
Melena is still the heir presumptive. Meaning: if anything happens to Elias or Katherine, the crown goes to her.
This changes everything.
Katherine never wanted the throne. Melena spent her entire life preparing for it.And now? They’re both trapped by blood.
And the public doesn’t even know they’re sisters yet.
### KATHERINE’S ARC:
Starts tracking the real versions of the poem.
Finally finds proof that the Empire rewrote its own history — and that the Sanctum has been cleaning up “elemental inconsistencies” for decades.
Learns that the Tournament isn’t just political theater — it’s a culling.
Starts hearing about Somnoria not as myth or rumor, but as a threat the Empire is preparing for.
#### Why She’s Tracking the Poems?
At this point, Katherine isn’t chasing the crown — she’s chasing the truth. After everything the Empire did to suppress her Dyad status, rewrite the Tournament footage, and cover up what actually happened with Atlas, she starts asking questions. Not out loud. Not where people can hear her. But internally? She needs to know why the Empire went this far to keep her fire hidden. And that question leads her back to something she thought was just history: the poem.
The Dreamwalker’s Writ, the Breathbound Law, the Elder’s Prayer — they all start to feel connected in ways no one ever taught her. So she starts digging. Quietly. Carefully. And every time she finds a record of someone like her — someone whose element didn’t match their assignment, or someone who Bound without permission — there’s a different version of the poem attached to their file. Every. Single. Time.
So now, it’s not just curiosity. It’s survival. Katherine starts tracking the poem across provinces, across languages, even across borders when whispers of Thalara and Somnoria start creeping into imperial reports. The verses don’t match. But the patterns do. And that’s when she realizes this poem isn’t prophecy — it’s a record of what the Empire erased. And if they went this hard to cover it up, she needs to know why. Before they do it again.
### MELENA’S ARC:
Her crown is slipping. Her certainty is gone.
She doesn’t know how to play a board that’s being redrawn in real time.
Starts realizing Katherine’s survival threatens her future, but her death might cost them the whole empire.
And when the Empress dies, Melena isn’t pushed aside — she’s trapped in place. A royal pawn, still standing, but no longer moving forward.
### CONTINENTAL ESCALATION:
Somnoria is now openly mentioned in government files, border reports, and whispered diplomatic threats.
There’s unrest in the western provinces. Something’s shifting in the old sea routes.
Katherine and Melena begin to realize: the next Tournament isn’t about Remnia anymore — it’s about preparing the Empire for war.
### WHAT THIS BOOK DOES:
| Event | Purpose |
| Empress dies | Forces succession crisis — and reveals how fragile the Lockwood line really is |
| Elias returns | Adds pressure and forces Katherine into direct royal spotlight |
| Katherine named heir apparent | Publicly solidifies her place — and privately confirms that the Empire knows the truth |
| Melena remains heir presumptive | She’s stuck — not dethroned, but clearly fading. Still needed. Still used. |
| Next Tournament is announced | Starts the countdown toward Book 4’s final reckoning |
# The Somnorian Archives
## Book 1: A Dream So Twisted
THEME: When the system decides who gets to dream, surviving it is the first rebellion.
CORE QUESTION: If your power was never supposed to exist, what do you do with it once it wakes up?
### WHERE WE’RE STARTING:
Reverie Hollow is a Dream Walker with outlawed abilities. She’s been walking dreams since she was twelve, and now works for Nightmare — the black-market trafficking ring that trades stolen dreams, memories, and signatures. Her entire life is built on pretending to be someone else: a scholarship kid, a mid-tier walker, just skilled enough to fly under the radar. But her power has always been too strong to stay hidden for long.
Everything shifts when she gets sent to infiltrate the mind of Professor Alexander Thorne, one of the most high-level researchers connected to the Guild. It’s supposed to be a clean job. Quick dream in, extract the intel, walk away. But Thorne’s dream is collapsing — and Reverie doesn’t just walk out with corrupted memories. She walks out with a piece of research that shouldn’t exist.
The formula references the Oneiros Prism, thought to be non-functional, and warns of an incoming celestial alignment that could destabilize the entire dream network.
That’s when Cassius Aether finds her.
Cassius isn’t a student. He’s an Associate Dream Master and heir to House Aether — one of the most powerful bloodlines in Somnoria. He’s already survived the Labyrinth of Stars. Already proven himself to the Guild. He shouldn’t need help from someone like Reverie. But the same misfires she’s been hiding? He’s experiencing them too.
Now they’re working together.Not because they want to — but because the system is starting to glitch, and they’re the only ones seeing it clearly.
### WHAT THEY UNCOVER:
The Guild has been covering up dream destabilizations linked to rare bloodlines and celestial alignment cycles that happen every seven years.
The Oneiros Prism — an ancient artifact once used to regulate conduit energy — is reacting to Reverie and Cassius together, even though their bloodlines should be incompatible.
Reverie’s walking ability doesn’t match any licensed dream class, and Cassius’s control is slipping every time she’s near.
Their connection is setting off warning systems inside the Labyrinth, and they both know if the Guild finds out, they’re done.
Nightmare isn’t just trafficking anymore — they’re tracking people like Reverie. And her name is already on their list.
### WHAT THIS BOOK SETS UP:
| Element | Impact |
| Reverie | She’s not trying to start a rebellion. She just wants to stay alive long enough to walk away. But now the network won’t let her. |
| Cassius | He’s been the Guild’s golden heir for years. But nothing in his training prepared him for someone like Reverie — or the way their magic overlaps. |
| The Prism | It's not dormant. It's reacting to them. And the last time this happened, people died and the Guild buried it. |
| The Guild | They’ve built an entire system off selective memory. If Reverie and Cassius blow it open, the whole economy burns with it. |
### DREAM SYSTEM + POLITICAL SETUP:
The Somnorian dream economy runs on licensed walking, memory curation, and limited dream-sharing. Only the Guild regulates dream use legally.
Conduits — people who naturally absorb and amplify dream energy — are considered too unstable to live freely.
The Council of Dream Masters runs enforcement, while Houses like Aether, Thorne, and others are political power players in high society.
Nightmare, the trafficking ring, is older than most people think — and it’s evolving. Quietly. Systematically. Preparing for something bigger.
### ENDPOINT:
By the end of Book One:
Reverie and Cassius can’t hide their connection anymore — not from each other, not from the Guild.
The Prism activates fully for the first time in years. And it does so because of them.
Thorne’s research proves the alignment is already starting, and the last time that happened, the dream network almost collapsed.
Nightmare resurfaces — not to erase them, but to recruit.
The Guild is scrambling. And on the other side of the continent, in Remnia? Magic starts to misfire.
## Book 2: A Fate Once Fractured
THEME: You don’t get to break a system like this without bleeding for it.
CORE QUESTION: Now that the Guild knows what they are, how do Reverie and Cassius survive being the threat the system has always warned about?
### WHERE WE’RE STARTING:
By the end of Book 1, Reverie and Cassius had already crossed the line. The Prism activated, the Guild covered it up, and Nightmare made their presence fully known. So Book 2 picks up with no more pretending.
Reverie and Cassius are together — emotionally, magically, and physically.Their bond is the only reason the Prism hasn’t fractured the network.But that connection is exactly what’s making them dangerous.
Everyone wants something from them:
The Council wants control.
Nightmare wants Reverie turned into a weapon.
Elara wants damage control.
And House Aether? They want Cassius to fall back in line — or disappear.
### REVERIE:
Her walking is unstable. She’s falling into dreams she never entered, waking up in places that don’t exist.
She’s finally remembering pieces of the experiments done on her as a child — and they tie directly to her father, who is very much alive and being used by Nightmare as a corrupted anchor.
She doesn’t just want to stop Nightmare anymore — she wants to burn the whole thing down.
But every time she uses her power at full strength, the network shifts — and the Prism shows her a future she’s not sure she survives.
### CASSIUS:
He’s done being a pawn for House Aether.
The Prism is bonded to both of them, and he knows Reverie’s the reason it woke up — but he’s the reason it’s not killing them.
He’s starting to see that his family’s legacy isn’t about protecting the dreamworld — it’s about ownership. Control. Silence.
And when the Guild starts blacklisting Conduits as “unstable dream threats,” Cassius is forced to pick a side — and it sure as hell isn’t theirs.
### WHAT THIS BOOK REALLY DOES:
| Plot Point | What It Breaks |
| The Guild labels Reverie and Cassius “Unverified Entities.” | Once that hits, they’re hunted. Not questioned. Not detained. Hunted. |
| Reverie’s father is confirmed alive — but twisted by Nightmare’s network. | She realizes she’s not the first. Just the only one who survived. |
| House Aether threatens to remove Cassius from all official records. | He becomes a ghost in the system. No name. No status. Just magic. |
| The Prism malfunctions after a mass dreamwalk event. | It tries to overwrite them. They fight back. It evolves. |
| The Labyrinth starts pulling in unregistered walkers. | The dream network isn’t just responding. It’s retaliating. |
### REVERIE + CASSIUS TOGETHER:
They’re past the push and pull.
They walk dreams side by side.
They train in secret using corrupted dreamscapes.
They are learning how to trigger controlled fractures — to weaponize what the Guild can’t contain.
And when the Prism pushes them too far, they choose each other over everything else.
### ENDING BEATS:
The Prism enters a new phase — no longer reactive. It starts calling other walkers.
Nightmare tries to extract Reverie’s father through the core — Cassius stops them, but not without consequence.
The Guild votes to issue a Containment Order. Reverie and Cassius are officially marked as existential threats.
Reverie sees a vision that shakes her — it’s not just Somnoria falling.The dream fractures are reaching Remnia.
## Book 3: These Hollowed Walls
THEME: You can’t fix a system built to disappear you. You either break it or get buried with it.
CORE QUESTION: Now that Reverie and Cassius are the ones holding the network together, what happens when the world starts pulling it apart?
### WHERE WE’RE STARTING:
The Containment Order is in effect.
The Guild has officially labeled Reverie and Cassius as unstable threats to the dream network — and they’re not wrong.
The Prism has evolved. It’s no longer reacting — it’s choosing.
And now it’s bleeding into places it shouldn’t.
Reverie’s dreams start glitching — but she’s not seeing broken walkscapes or old memories anymore.
She’s seeing Remnia.
She doesn’t know what that place is or why she’s seeing it. She just knows the people feel real. Especially the girl made of fire and frost.
Reverie starts dreaming of Katherine Lockwood — and it feels like a warning.
### CASSIUS:
It’s not just Reverie. He’s been glitching too.
He dreams of drowning.
Of a Binding that doesn’t belong to him.
Of a sword he doesn’t know how to use… until he does.
He doesn’t tell Reverie at first — because what is he supposed to say? That he’s having memories he never lived?
That when he looks at her, sometimes it feels like someone else’s grief is bleeding through?
And then he dreams of Katherine screaming over a boy’s body — flames breaking out of her hands, and a prayer in a language he doesn’t recognize but understands perfectly.
He wakes up and says,
“I think someone brought me back. I just don’t think it was me.”
Cassius is dreaming Atlas’s memories.
And the dream network is officially out of time.
### WHAT THIS BOOK BREAKS:
| Thing That Breaks | Why It Matters |
| The Guild initiates the Hollowed Walls Protocol | A last-resort purge of the entire dream system. If the Prism isn’t contained, everything goes offline. |
| Nightmare fractures | Half of them join Reverie. The other half tries to take her out before she destabilizes the system for good. |
| The Labyrinth starts responding to Remnian magic | It shouldn’t be possible — but now it’s syncing with a continent that doesn’t even exist on Somnoria’s maps. |
### REVERIE & CASSIUS:
They’re bonded. Period. Emotionally, magically, cosmically.And the Prism no longer responds to them as individuals — it mirrors their unity.
They:
Walk through corrupted dreamspace side by side
Unlock ancestral dreamprints from past conduits
Start seeing visions of what came before the Guild even existed
And learn that they weren’t born to serve this system.They were born to replace it
### ENDGAME:
Reverie accesses the deep core of the Hollowed Walls and finds the Prism’s final lock: a dreamprint that doesn’t belong to any known bloodline.
Cassius opens the Prism at full force — and the Labyrinth sends out a signal that spans beyond Somnoria.
Reverie wakes up in the final chapter from a dream where Katherine Lockwood says her name.
And across the sea, Cassius’s dreamprint pulses inside Atlas Tolliver’s body.
# The Shellkeeper’s Shores
## Book 1: The Sea Will Keep
Starring: Azrine Thalyn Virell & Leviathan Caelis Dren(Little Mermaid x Atlantis x Greek Mythology with fated mates & rising gods)
THEME: When legacy is buried in silence, it will rise in waves.

Thalara was drowned on purpose.Its relics shattered. Its bloodlines erased.But the ocean didn’t forget.

Her job was supposed to be simple: listen, remember, and carry the stories forward. But the shells are silent in her hands — and the elders think that means she’s broken.
They’re wrong.Azrine doesn’t hear the past.She hears what was never meant to be remembered.

It’s a threat. A warning carved into prophecy and sealed in the salt-steel temple he’s never been allowed to enter.
But when a storm wrecks a coastal defense wall, and a girl washes up on the Skyhold’s private beach whispering forbidden verses…The relic branded with his name unlocks for the first time.And Cael can’t pretend anymore.

Azrine and Cael have dreamed of each other for years — but always as shadows.When they meet for real, everything breaks open:
Her voice activates the Leviathan’s seal.
His name wakes the shells in her hands.
And together, they trigger a chain reaction that sends ripples through every region on the continent.

# The Mourning Cycle
## Book 1: The Mourning Verse
Book One of The Mourning CycleStarring: Isola & Silas(a Hades + Persephone coded modern mythology retelling soaked in grief, prophecy, and resurrection)
THEME: Grief isn’t what follows death. It’s what shapes what comes after.
CORE QUESTION: What do you become when the world says you were never supposed to come back?
### SETTING: The Underside
The Underside is a territory erased from maps, buried under centuries of silence and secrets. 
It’s not beneath the world — it’s below the memory of it.
There’s no light here unless you bring your own. Magic exists — but it’s not power. It’s penance. The most feared gift? Resurrection. And it’s illegal.
The Order of Silence controls the region — a church-military order tasked with maintaining spiritual control, suppressing prophecy, and ensuring the Mourning Verse is never spoken again.
But it’s already too late.
### ISOLA:
She doesn’t talk much.But when she does, the world listens.
She died when she was a child.No one talks about it. Not even her.
She came back different — quiet, cold, always humming something no one taught her.
That something? The Mourning Verse.
She was told it was a trauma echo. A hallucination.But she knows better.She hears the bones hum back.
Isola works tending the graves behind the Temple of the First Silence.But lately? Graves have started shifting.And one — just one — has cracked open from the inside.
### SILAS:
Silas doesn't sleep anymore.Not since he came back.
He was dead for three years. Documented. Sealed. Mourning rites performed.
And then he woke up. No warning. No pulse. No explanation.
He doesn’t remember how he died.He only remembers her voice calling him back.
He doesn’t know her name. Not yet. But he’s been looking.Because whatever woke him? Wants him to find her.
When he walks into the temple and hears her humming?
His first words are:
"You used to sing that in my coffin."
### DYNAMIC:
They don’t meet. They remember each other.Not with facts — with feeling.
Silas knows how she feels when she’s about to cry.
Isola knows how he moves when he’s about to run.
They don’t fall in love. They realize they already did — before they ever had the chance to live.
“I don't think we're cursed. I think we're still unfinished.”
### FULL PLOT OVERVIEW:
#### ACT I: The Grave Cracks
Isola finds a broken grave in the cemetery she keeps.Not vandalized — opened from the inside.
The nameplate is burned off, but the sigil below it matches one she’s been drawing in her sleep.
Silas arrives in the city shortly after, led by dreams he hasn’t told anyone about.When he hears her humming, he freezes. It’s the same sound from inside the crypt he clawed out of.
That night, the Mourning Verse activates a bone relic hidden deep beneath the temple.Something ancient wakes. The Order panics. They start watching Isola.
#### ACT II: The Death Wasn’t the End
Isola starts seeing faces in the graveglass. Names that were erased from the death records.
Silas touches the same relic and sees a prophetic version of his death — except this time, Isola dies with him.
They discover a sealed room under the temple that isn’t on any blueprint.It contains a mirror slate with a message burned into it:
“When four awaken, the curse will break. The mourned shall rise. The empire will remember.”
The Order tries to arrest them. Silas bleeds ash when they cut him.Isola sings — really sings — and the floor cracks beneath their feet.
#### ACT III: The Prophecy Returns
A girl long-dead speaks through Isola at the altar.She calls Silas by his true name — one no one else knows.The Mourning Verse finishes itself in Isola’s voice.
The temple lights go out. The mirror cracks. The prophecy rebrands itself in flame:
“The fire has spoken. The dream has risen.Now the dead must choose: silence or return.”
Silas and Isola don’t run.They step forward.Together.
#### ENDING:
The relic names them: “The Dead Mirror”Not because they died.But because they're the mirror that no one ever wanted to survive.
The Order tries to claim their resurrection was a mistake.Isola speaks the Mourning Verse aloud again in front of the Sanctum.
The bones in the temple sing back.A grave bursts open in the distance.And someone who shouldn’t be alive walks out of it.
The dead are listening.The prophecy has begun.
## Book 2: The Blood Between
THEME: When blood connects you to what should’ve stayed buried, how do you know who you really are?
CORE QUESTION: If you were never supposed to survive, are you allowed to change what comes next?
### TONE SHIFT FROM BOOK ONE:
Book One whispered.
Book Two bleeds.
The verse has been sung. The mirror has cracked.And now the world knows Isola and Silas exist — and that alone is a problem.
This isn’t just about love anymore.It’s about what love resurrected — and who’s afraid of it.
### WHERE WE’RE STARTING:
The events of The Mourning Verse shook the temple to its foundation — literally.Now the Order of Silence is in full crisis mode.
Bone relics have started glowing again.
Graves are no longer sealed.
Spirits are whispering prophecies that haven’t been heard in centuries.
And in the middle of it all?Isola and Silas.Branded. Hunted. Bound.And still trying to figure out who they were before they died — and why they came back at all.
### ISOLA:
Her voice is stronger now — it calls the dead without her meaning to.
The verse inside her is evolving — she starts hearing new stanzas in dreams.
She’s begun to remember something she shouldn’t: a life that wasn’t hers.
A life where she was already singing this prophecy… for someone else.
She doesn’t know if it’s reincarnation, inherited memory, or something worse.She only knows that Silas has been in those visions, every time.
### SILAS:
His body is changing. Not faster or stronger — stranger.
Blood doesn’t feel like blood anymore. It feels like ink. Like script.
He can’t die, but he also can’t touch certain things without triggering memories that weren’t his.
He starts drawing symbols in his sleep.They match what’s carved into the original Order tombs.And the only way he could know them… is if he was one of the first mirrorbound.
### PLOT STRUCTURE:
#### ACT I: The Blood Speaks
The bones from the temple have begun whispering names.
Some of those names belong to people still alive.
Isola and Silas are given a choice:Flee to the Underside’s edge, or enter the catacombs to find the sealed relic the Mourning Verse was originally carved into.
They go down.
#### ACT II: The Mirror Cracks Wider
They discover that their bond — their resurrection — wasn’t an accident.It was an interruption of a prophecy meant to crown someone else.
Someone else was meant to survive.
Someone else died in their place.
And now? That someone wants them back.
Silas begins to remember his death — all of it. It wasn’t random. It was part of a ritual.
#### ACT III: Blood Doesn’t Lie
A third mirror is found. Not fire. Not dream. Not sea.But one marked with bone and crown.
Isola sings the second stanza of the Mourning Verse.The moment she does, Silas bleeds black and drops.
He wakes up hours later with someone else’s voice in his throat.
He’s carrying a spirit now — not possessed, but merged.And that spirit?It’s the one who wrote the prophecy in the first place.
### ENDING:
The Order finds them again and tries to separate them permanently.Not with death. With binding.
But when they break the bond, the spirit inside Silas unleashes a memory-flash that shows them what’s really been hidden.
A fifth mirror.One that isn’t part of the prophecy.One that was erased completely.
And it wore Isola’s face.
“You weren’t just supposed to return.You were supposed to be forgotten.”
## Book 3: The Hollowed Curse
THEME: How far will you go to unwrite a curse that was never yours to carry?
CORE QUESTION: If love broke the rules of death, where do you go when death tries to take it back?
### WHERE WE START:
The Mourning Verse is rewritten
The Order of Silence has fractured
The dead are still — but Silas is not stable
Something in him is unraveling — slowly, but surely
The resurrection magic used to return him was incomplete — and the power source is dying
Isola is not letting him go.Not again.Not like this.
So when she learns there’s a place beyond the Underside — a province that deals in living memory and resonance magic — she decides to find it.
She’s going to Somnoria.
### WHAT THIS BOOK IS REALLY ABOUT:
This is not a “break the curse” book.This is a “go find the ones who survived it” book.
Isola starts this story looking for a solution.She ends it by discovering that there are others like her.And that the reason her love was erased…is because it came first.
### PLOT STRUCTURE:
#### ACT I: The Afterburn
Silas is alive, but unstable — his body is caught between timelines
Isola begins researching resonance magic, memory stitching, and energy rebalancing — all of which are banned in the Underside
She finds a carved shell fragment in the bone vault — it hums when Silas touches it
It doesn't belong to this world.It belongs to Thalara.
#### ACT II: To Save Him, She Leaves
Isola crosses into forbidden territory — chasing a rumor of a girl who walked out of the sea with prophecy on her skin
She begins having visions of Azrine — not clear, but flickering, like memory echoes
She finds mention of a place where souls are reanchored, not resurrected — a province governed by dreamkeepers
Somnoria.
She leaves the Underside to find it — and in doing so, fractures a boundary no one was supposed to cross.
#### ACT III: All Mirrors Are Lit
In Somnoria, Isola meets a Keeper of the Prism — they don’t know her, but they recognize the verse
They warn her: if Silas is unraveling, it’s because his tether was incompleteHe needs more than magic. He needs memory. Connection. Legacy.
In her desperation, Isola ties his essence to hers in a mirrorbonding rite designed to stabilize power between lovers
It works — temporarily.But it sends a signal to all the mirrors.
And for the first time ever, all four mirrors pulse at once.
#### ENDING:
Silas is not only alive — he’s finally whole
His power no longer bleeds — it listens
Isola returns home to the Underside, but she’s not the girl who left itShe’s a bearer of two worlds now
The final page ends with her standing at the temple ruins, her hand in his, whispering:
“You lived.Now it’s time we find the ones who helped us do it.”
They turn to the horizon. Because Their Reckoning is coming.And the world will finally remember what it tried to bury.
