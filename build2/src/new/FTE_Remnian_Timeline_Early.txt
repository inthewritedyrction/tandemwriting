Timeline of the Empire of Remnia
Year 1 ER: Founding of an Empire
Empress Eden Genesis Hastings, newly crowned, focuses on consolidating her rule, establishing stability, and setting the groundwork for a lasting empire. The initial period is marked by both enthusiasm and apprehension. As the empire’s first year unfolds, Eden navigates the challenges of uniting diverse regions and people under a single banner.
Founding of the Empire:
Proclamation Ceremony: A grand ceremony is held in London Town, with representatives from each province attending. Eden delivers a speech outlining her vision for the new empire, emphasizing unity, progress, and prosperity.
Capital: London Town is chosen as the capital due to its strategic location and existing infrastructure. The city undergoes immediate enhancements to serve as the administrative and cultural heart of the empire.
Administrative Structure:
Formation of Provincial Governance:
Governing Councils: Each province is given a governing council responsible for local administration and coordination with the central government.
Regional Governors: Appointments are made for regional governors who oversee the day-to-day affairs in their respective provinces.
Administrative Buildings: Construction of key administrative buildings, such as the Imperial Palace, Ministry of Finance, and the Provincial Offices, begins in London Town.
Legal Framework:
Constitution Drafting:
Constitutional Assembly: A special assembly is convened to draft the empire’s constitution. Key figures from various provinces are involved to ensure broad representation.
Rights and Responsibilities: The constitution outlines the rights of citizens, the powers of the Empress, and the structure of the legislative and judicial branches.
Initial Legislation: The first set of laws focuses on taxation, trade regulations, and public safety. Special attention is given to laws that promote unity and prevent regional conflicts.
Military and Defense:
Imperial Guard Formation:
Recruitment and Training: Recruitment for the Imperial Guard begins, with a focus on selecting skilled and loyal individuals. Initial training camps are set up.
Headquarters: The Guard’s headquarters is established in London Town, equipped with state-of-the-art facilities for training and strategy planning.
Border Fortifications: Construction of fortifications along the empire’s borders is prioritized. Key locations for these fortifications include vulnerable areas identified during early security assessments.
Economic Initiatives:
Economic Stabilization:
Trade Agreements: Negotiations for trade agreements with neighboring countries are initiated to boost the economy and secure essential resources.
Infrastructure Development: Investment in infrastructure projects such as roads, bridges, and ports to facilitate trade and communication across the empire.
Currency and Banking:
Currency Design: A new currency is designed with security features and symbols representing the empire’s values.
Central Bank: Establishment of the Imperial Central Bank to oversee the new currency and manage the empire’s finances.
Cultural and Social Developments:
Cultural Promotion:
Festivals and Events: Organization of cultural festivals, including a grand opening festival in London Town, to celebrate the empire’s creation and promote cultural unity.
Cultural Institutions: Establishment of institutions such as the Imperial Museum and Cultural Center to preserve and promote the empire’s heritage.
Education System:
Educational Reforms: Development of a national curriculum and standards for education, with the aim of improving literacy and providing quality education across the empire.
Founding of Schools: Initial funding and construction of schools and educational facilities in major cities and towns.
Diplomatic Relations:
Formation of Alliances:
Initial Treaties: Signing of treaties with neighboring nations to establish diplomatic relations, trade agreements, and mutual defense pacts.
Diplomatic Corps: Creation of a diplomatic corps to manage relations with foreign governments and handle diplomatic negotiations.
International Presence:
Embassies and Consulates: Establishment of embassies and consulates in key foreign capitals to enhance the empire’s international presence and influence.
Challenges and Responses:
Internal Resistance:
Discontent Management: Addressing discontent in regions resistant to the new empire through diplomacy, negotiations, and, if necessary, strategic interventions.
Reconciliation Efforts: Implementation of policies to promote reconciliation and integration of diverse groups into the new empire.
External Threats:
Security Measures: Monitoring and responding to any external threats or border skirmishes through enhanced surveillance and military readiness.
Defense Alliances: Strengthening defense alliances with friendly nations to deter potential aggressors.

## Year 13 ER (2038) – Dawn of New Legacies
Year 13 ER marks a period of significant change and development within the Empire of Remnia. The year is characterized by the passage of crucial legislation and the birth of notable new members of the imperial and royal families.
### Dukedom Act of 2038 ER
In a move to consolidate the unity of the empire and honor the heritage of its royal and imperial families, the Dukedom Act of 2038 ER is enacted. This landmark legislation grants heirs of royal and imperial families the titles of Duke or Duchess of the provinces their parents hail from. The act is intended to strengthen ties between the central authority and the provinces, ensuring that the ruling families remain closely connected to their regions of origin.

## Year 14 ER (2039): The Dawn of New Institutions
In Year 14 ER, Remnia witnesses the establishment of its first formal parliamentary system, a transformative step in its governance. This new system is designed to bring greater democratic representation and ensure a more inclusive approach to decision-making. The Parliament consists of representatives elected from each of Remnia’s provinces, allowing diverse voices from across the empire to contribute to national legislation.
### Remnian Parliament:
Structure: The Parliament is bicameral, consisting of the House of Commons and the House of Lords. The House of Commons is composed of representatives elected directly by the citizens of each province. The House of Lords includes appointed members, such as influential nobles and prominent figures from various sectors, to provide expertise and oversight.
Functions: The Parliament’s primary functions include debating proposed laws, reviewing existing legislation, and addressing the concerns of different regions. It serves as a platform for discussing policies that impact the empire, promoting transparency and accountability in governance.
Sessions: Parliament sessions are held quarterly, with special sessions convened as needed to address urgent matters. Each session includes debates, committee reviews, and voting on proposed bills.
Impact: The introduction of the Parliament aims to enhance the representation of provincial interests and facilitate a more balanced and fair legislative process. It marks a significant shift from the previous centralized governance model, reflecting Remnia’s commitment to democratic principles and regional autonomy.
### New Laws and Regulations:
Several new laws are introduced in Year 14 ER to support the evolving needs of Remnia’s expanding empire:
#### Property Rights Law:
Objective: To clarify and protect property ownership and land use across the empire.
Details: This law establishes clear guidelines for property rights, including registration, transfer, and dispute resolution. It aims to enhance legal certainty for property owners and facilitate smoother transactions in the real estate market.
#### Business Regulations Act:
Objective: To streamline and regulate business operations, promoting economic growth and fair competition.
Details: The Act introduces standardized procedures for business registration, taxation, and compliance. It includes measures to prevent monopolistic practices and ensure a level playing field for enterprises of all sizes.
#### Urban Development Code:
Objective: To guide the planning and development of urban areas, ensuring sustainable growth and efficient use of resources.
Details: This code provides regulations for zoning, infrastructure development, and environmental protection. It aims to support the expansion of cities while preserving natural habitats and maintaining quality of life for residents.
#### Public Health and Safety Regulations:
Objective: To enhance public health and safety standards across the empire.
Details: The regulations cover areas such as healthcare access, sanitation, and emergency response. They include provisions for improving medical facilities, ensuring food safety, and preparing for natural disasters and health crises.

## Year 17 ER (2042): The Imperian War 
The year 2042 ER is marked by the eruption of intense conflict within the Empire of Remnia, ignited by a series of tragic events and escalating tensions between the elemental factions.
### March 11: Kidnapped
Event: In a shocking development, individuals from eight sectors are kidnapped and held in Verdant by an anti-elementalist group. This group's primary objective is to eradicate those with ancient magical abilities, believing that elemental magic is a threat to their vision of a pure society.
Details: The kidnappings occur covertly, leaving Empress Eden and Emperor Stephen unaware of the immediate danger. The abducted individuals include influential figures and their families, who are targeted due to their association with elemental magic.
### April 8: Genocide
Event: Following the discovery of the kidnappings, it is revealed that over 800,000 children have been systematically abducted, subjected to horrific experiments, and killed over the past seven years. This atrocity is revealed as the largest genocide in the history of the empire.
Details: The genocide shocks the empire, exposing the extent of the anti-elementalist group's brutality. The revelation stirs deep outrage and sorrow among the populace, fueling a demand for justice and retribution.
### May 28: Imperian War I Begins
Event: In response to the genocide and the threat posed by the anti-elementalist group, parents of young elementalists and other supporters declare war against the perpetrators. The conflict, known as Imperian War I, witnesses the mobilization of over 270,000 individuals.
Details: The war is marked by fierce battles and widespread devastation, with both sides suffering significant casualties. The fighting lasts for eleven months, profoundly impacting the empire's stability and unity. The war is characterized by its intensity and the deep divisions it creates among the elemental factions.
### October 27: Removal
Event: As a measure to address the escalating tensions and to provide a structured outlet for resolving disputes, Empress Eden approves the initiation of the first Chess Tournament.
Details: The Chess Tournament is established as a diplomatic and strategic tool designed to offer a platform for the factions to compete in a controlled and formal environment. It aims to promote intellectual engagement and foster a spirit of cooperation and reconciliation among the warring factions. This step is seen as a significant move towards mending the fractured relationships and restoring some semblance of peace within the empire.
