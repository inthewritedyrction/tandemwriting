## Level A — Production Control

The Companion is trustworthy only if every sentence carries its own authority. These rules decide how a claim earns a place on the page and how it must be worded once it's there.

### A1. What the Companion is, and isn't

The Tandem Companion is a private writer-room reference for Danyelle's four-land universe: canon archive, continuity system, and drafting partner in one printed book. It teaches first and asks second.

It is not a fan wiki, a lore encyclopedia, a marketing synopsis, a prompt book, or a workbook waiting to be filled in. It never outranks the manuscripts. When drafted prose deliberately changes an entry, the entry changes.

### A2. Source authority

Authority is domain-specific. A source can control one kind of fact and have no say over another.

1. **Explicit author rulings** (Canon Decision Log, TCD-001 onward) control every domain they address.
2. **Current manuscript pages** control what has happened on-page: POV, scene chronology, revealed identity, performed magic, relationships, immediate consequences.
3. **Locked constraints** (*Novel Constraints*) control intended premise and undrafted core engine, as corrected by rulings.
4. **Current outlines/chapter plans** control undrafted plot only after their generation is confirmed, and never overwrite drafted scenes.
5. **Current book-specific worldbuilding, character, magic, and institutional files** control where the manuscript is silent and no ruling conflicts.
6. **Shared Tandem lore** controls by claim only.
7. **Supporting artifacts** (timelines, placement tables, genealogy workbooks, poems, quote maps) control their narrow domain after consistency checks.
8. **Marketing files** describe positioning; they establish nothing.
9. **Legacy drafts and plans** are creative history. They cannot fill a canon gap.
10. **TKC files** have zero Tandem authority.

Filenames and dates don't confer authority. "FINAL," "Official," "New," and a recent year are labels, not rulings. *FTE 2025 FINAL* is Legacy; *New FTE Chapters* predates the modern Remnia execution.

### A3. Status system and permitted wording

Each status governs the grammar a sentence may use. This is the single most important production rule: a status marker next to declarative prose that overstates it is still a canon error.

| Marker | Meaning | Permitted wording | Forbidden wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **\[C\] CANON** | Ruling or controlling source | Plain declarative present tense. Cite the TCD or source. | Hedging that makes settled canon look optional |
| **CURRENT DRAFTED TEXT** | Established on-page in the controlling generation | "On-page, …" "As drafted, …" | Treating it as immune to revision |
| **SUPPORTED / PROVISIONAL** | Current plan, bible, or artifact not yet drafted | "The outline plans…" "The blueprint proposes…" | Present-tense narration of events as if they happened |
| **\[P\] POSSIBLE** | Live candidate or recovery idea | "A candidate is…" "One viable reading…" | "Is," "will," "does" about the story world |
| **\[?\] OPEN** | Unresolved, author decision needed if it becomes necessary | State what is fixed, then what is not | Filling the gap with a plausible answer |
| **\[X\] CONFLICT** | Current sources disagree, no ruling | Name both sources and both claims | Picking the nicer version silently |
| **\[R\] RESEARCH** | Needs outside factual research | State the research question | Presenting a guess as fact |
| **LEGACY / SUPERSEDED** | Creative history | Past tense, source named: "The 2025 FINAL had…" | Using it to answer a current question |

Markers attach to claims, not pages. One dossier page can hold \[C\], \[P\], and \[?\] claims side by side.

### A4. Claim construction

Every factual statement about Tandem traces to an explicit ruling, a controlling source within its domain, or a non-conflicting supporting source. Nothing else. When none applies, the Companion marks the point open.

High-traffic or cross-book claims get a provenance record:

`Claim | Status | Book/domain | Controlling source/ruling | Supporting source(s) | Superseded source(s) | Knowledge state | Last reviewed | Notes`

In printed pages, provenance appears as a margin note ("TCD-063; 3SL draft ch. 22") rather than a full record. Full records live in the appendices.

### A5. Knowledge states

No later revelation may travel backward into an earlier character's head. Every entry that involves a secret, identity, reveal, or historical truth separates these lanes as needed:

- **Objective truth** — what is true in the universe.
- **Author knowledge** — ruled or known by Danyelle, whether or not any character knows it.
- **Reader knowledge** — what a reader knows at a given point.
- **Character knowledge** — what a named character knows, and when.
- **Character belief** — what they think is true when it's wrong or incomplete.
- **Institutional/public version** — what Houses, the Sanctum, the Synod, Skyhold, schools, or governments teach.
- **Suppressed/rewritten** — sealed, censored, misfiled, mythologized, redacted, lost.

Cross-land knowledge also uses the six TCD-016 levels: existence, location, functional, route, historical, true Tandem knowledge. None implies another.

In-world language survives as perspective, never as cosmology. A Somnorian calling Remnia "another world" is recorded as Somnorian framing (TCD-015). House Aether calling the Prism a "cage" is recorded as House Aether's interpretation (TCD-038). Caelum calling Melena his "asset" is recorded as Caelum's framing (TCD-043).

### A6. Cross-book connection test

A shared bloodline, artifact, phrase, ritual, institution, effect, event, route, symbol, prophecy, character, or place counts as a Tandem connection only when a source or ruling supports it. Similar imagery isn't evidence. Temporal proximity isn't causation (TCD-054). Repeated nouns aren't shared ontology (the Tavian "fragment" is not a Prism fragment, TCD-039).

### A8. Open material protocol

Open stays open. Where an open point sits near canon, the page carries an **OPEN DEVELOPMENT** box with four lines:

- **Fixed:** what rulings already lock.
- **Undecided:** the exact open question.
- **Why it matters:** what it would change.
- **Decide by:** the actual drafting point that forces it ("before the 3SL climax draft"), or "safe to defer."

Never ask Danyelle to resolve something just because the Companion reached that page. Only stop the whole section when the question truly prevents accurate writing.

### A9. Canon decision protocol

When a page truly cannot proceed, use a **TANDEM CANON DECISION** block: Issue, Sources, options A–D plus E ("Something else / I want to explain"), a one-line implication under each option, and a labeled Recommendation only when evidence strongly favors one path. Maximum five at once, preferably one to three. A recommendation is never canon until ruled. Asking a question assigns no TCD number.

### A10. Update protocol

When a new ruling lands or drafted prose changes something:

1. Log it in the Canon Decision Log with date, supersession, and downstream implications.
2. Update the High-Impact Conflict Register if it resolves or opens a conflict.
3. Update every page listed in the ruling's "affected pages" field.
4. Move superseded claims to the Legacy Recovery Index rather than deleting them.
5. Re-run the integrity checks (A12) on touched pages.

### A11. No-invention list

The Companion never supplies: mottos, heraldry, sigils, maps, institution seals, quotations, epigraphs, faux archival documents, historical dates, family crests, artifact inscriptions, or magical diagrams presented as fact. Decorative graphics must derive from canon, be clearly non-canonical decoration, or be separately approved.

### A12. Integrity checks (run on every section before it's called done)

- No Legacy claim presented as canon.
- No \[P\] or SUPPORTED claim in declarative story-world tense.
- No conflict silently harmonized.
- No knowledge promoted backward.
- No symmetry assumed across the four lands.
- No duplicate or contradictory table.
- No invented canon.
- No open question phrased as settled.
- No TKC content imported.

### A13. Required development records

The audit's development records are canon-control tools, not prerequisites. Each is built inside the section where it earns its keep, filled with what's already established, with open fields visibly marked. No empty forms produced merely because a record exists on the list.

### A14. Design constraints carried forward

Content outranks layout. Design may build hierarchy, badges, sidebars, navigation, writing space, and book identities. Design may not rewrite canon, cut distinctions, resolve open points, merge truth with belief, convert \[P\] to \[C\], make Legacy look current, shrink tables to illegibility, or add decorative lore. Oversized tables split across pages instead of shrinking. No horizontal separator rules in the drafted copy.

## Level A — Voice & Page Style Bible

The Companion sounds like someone who has read every manuscript, knows which details are load-bearing, and is sitting next to Danyelle while she drafts. Confident where canon is settled. Candid where it isn't.

### V1. Voice principles

- **Declarative first.** Say what's true, then why it matters, then where it bites in drafting.
- **Specific over general.** Name the character, chapter, ruling, and consequence.
- **Distinctions are the product.** The best sentences separate two things a drafter might blur.
- **Comfortable with not knowing.** "We don't know this yet" is a complete, respectable sentence.
- **Occasionally sharp.** A short warning beats a long caveat.
- **Contractions welcome.** Fragments only when they carry rhythm or emphasis.
- **Never homogenize the books.** Quoted or described manuscript material keeps its own voice; the Companion's voice stays out of the characters' mouths.

### V2. Voice in practice

Weak (generic coach): *"Remy's death is a pivotal moment. As a writer, you may want to think about how Melena feels about what happened."*

Strong (writer-room): *"The gang leader kills Remy. Melena's Fire doesn't. She'll carry it as though it did, and FTE is allowed to let her, but this page keeps the forensic record apart from her guilt."* \[C\] TCD-063

Weak (wiki): *"Katherine is a rare Fire/Frost Dyad, like Zephyr."*

Strong: *"The Sanctum would call Katherine a Dyad. It'd be wrong. Zephyr is a modern Fire/Frost Dyad; Katherine is one older, less-specialized capacity that organized itself around Frost. Fire isn't a second channel waiting behind a door. It's where the capacity reroutes when the Frost configuration fails."* \[C\] TCD-049, TCD-058

### V3. Banned moves

- "As a writer, you may want to consider…" / "Think about how…" / "Ask yourself…"
- Strings of rhetorical questions.
- Inspirational filler, vague praise, "lesson learned" endings.
- Polished transitions that say nothing ("Moving on to…", "It's worth noting that…").
- Overly symmetrical prose and forced threes.
- "This page outlines…" openers. First sentence is the point.
- Invented lore offered as texture.
- Marketing-synopsis summaries of the books.

### V4. The teach-first pattern

The default page movement is **Reference → Why it matters → Writing application**. Writing space appears only after the page has taught something, and every writing prompt is tied to a specific canon object ("Map a new Severance against the five known laws"), never a generic one ("Brainstorm Graveshand ideas").

### V5. Page type catalog

Pages don't share one template. Function picks the type.

| Page type | Use it for | Reference vs writing space |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explainer | Teaching a system or concept (Graveshand, Convergence) | Mostly reference; short application at the end |
| Dossier | A character, institution, or entity | Mostly reference; margin space for notes |
| Comparison spread | Two things readers blur (Prism vs Leviathan; Katherine vs Zephyr) | Table-led; brief analysis under it |
| Truth vs Belief page | Secrets, identities, reveals | Lane table; drafting notes column |
| Continuity Translation page | One event as later books remember it | Lane table; contradiction-risk column |
| Beat sheet | A locked sequence that's still undrafted (Remy's death, the forfeit) | Fixed beats marked \[C\]; open beats marked \[?\]; writing space beside open beats |
| Ladder / state model | Escalating mechanics (Bond Mechanics Ladder, Convergence states, Nightmare awakening) | Diagram or stepped table; notes space |
| Matrix / tracker | Knowledge, chronology, routes, symptoms | Structured fields; half pre-filled from canon |
| Ledger | Two-sided exchanges (Caelum/Melena leverage) | Pre-filled rows plus blank rows |
| Open Development page | A cluster of open choices in one area | Fixed / Undecided / Why / Decide-by, then space |
| Status & provenance sheet | Source inventories, decision logs | All reference |

### V6. Recurring page furniture

- **Status badge** beside every claim that isn't obviously \[C\] from context. On mixed pages, badge every claim.
- **Margin provenance** in small type: ruling or source ("TCD-044 · 3SL draft").
- **GUARDRAIL box** for locked constraints a drafter could accidentally break ("The tether is never evidence of consent.").
- **NOT THE SAME AS callout** for paired terms readers merge (Nightmare organization / ancient Nightmare).
- **OPEN DEVELOPMENT box** (Fixed / Undecided / Why it matters / Decide by).
- **TANDEM CANON DECISION block**, only where a page truly can't proceed.
- **Cross-reference line** at the section foot: "See also: §12 Convergence; §28 ADST Climax Packet."

### V7. Density and length

This is a working book; subjects that need sustained paragraphs get them. When content overflows, adjust layout, split to a second page, move detail to a sidebar, or convert to a table. Never delete meaning to fit a page. Paragraphs stay print-readable: roughly three to five sentences.

### V8. Tables

Use tables when comparison or continuity is the point: objective truth vs belief, intended plan vs outcome, inheritance vs conditioning vs authorization, chronology, jurisdiction, route class, actor knowledge. Don't table everything. A table that would need "n/a" in most cells is the wrong shape.

### V9. Writing space

Writing space is sized to the expected response, never a full page of empty lines by default. Every blank is preceded by a specific task. Trackers arrive half-filled with established canon so the writer extends rather than starts from zero.

### V10. Book identities (design direction only, no canonical symbols)

Each book gets a distinct visual register for navigation, drawn from its established setting rather than invented iconography: the Underside's vertical descent for 3SL, the Tournament board and provincial structure for FTE, the Dream Network for ADST, sea pressure and depth zones for TSWK. Final motifs need Danyelle's approval before any appear in print.

## Production Master TOC

The Companion runs in seven Parts plus appendices. Book dossiers now close with their own draft-forward packet, so each packet sits beside the book it serves instead of in a separate Part.
