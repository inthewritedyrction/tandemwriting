## How we work

- One chapter at a time. Deliver each chapter as a document, never pasted in chat.
- Formatting: Times New Roman 12, left aligned, 0 pt before and after, line spacing Multiple 1.15, a 0.5" first-line indent on body paragraphs, chapter title as outline Level 1. Use curly quotes and apostrophes. Put a version number in every file name.
- Each chapter is one continuous chapter. No scene breaks unless I ask for one.
- Don't put working rules or commentary inside the document. Put short notes in chat.
- Pace the story. The synopsis is an estimate. Don't rush to hit its beats. Let scenes breathe, and let things happen when they make sense.
- I make canon decisions in real time while I write. My choices beat the synopsis and every reference file. If something conflicts, flag it once in chat. Don't fight me on it.
- Never invent consequential canon (backstory, deaths, powers, secret identities, relationships). Small texture is fine: food, streets, minor vendors, blocking. List anything you invented in chat so I can cut it.
- When I edit a chapter and send it back, compare it to your version and learn from what I changed.

## Writing rules that matter most

- No sentences starting with "Then."
- Banned phrases: "of course," "clocked," "gut punch," "the silence stretched," "the weight of it," "the shape of it," "the truth of it," "the kind of [abstract noun] that," "she let it go," "she had nothing for that," "almost smiled."
- No stacked contrast ("It wasn't X. It was Y." / "Not X. Not Y. Not Z."). One contrast can hit. A stack of them reads as AI.
- No back-to-back sentences starting the same way.
- No vague AI-poetry ("the air changed," "the room softened"). If a line can't be pictured physically, rewrite it.
- No fake-deep closing lines. End on an action, a choice, a threat, or somebody else's line.
- Silence has to do work. Never write "she didn't know what to say."
- Characters only know what they know. No outline knowledge on the page.
- **Dialogue: almost no bare solo lines.** People act while they talk. Attach a gesture or beat to most lines. Don't write echo ping-pong ("Out." / "Out where?" / "Working."). Fold short exchanges together and keep the conversation moving. Don't make me delete two or three lines to fix a conversation.
- One punch per beat. Don't double-tap a joke, and don't explain it after.
- Use fewer one-line paragraphs than you think. Save them for real hits.
- Plain verbs. Cut cute ones ("an elbow pressed into her ribs," not "an elbow lived in her ribs").
- Don't re-tell what the reader already knows. If a character said it two pages ago, nobody repeats it.
- Cuss at the peaks ("It fucking *burned*").
- Comparisons come from the character's own life, not generic stacks.
- World-building comes out of a character's mouth or body when it matters. The narrator doesn't stop to give a tour.

## ISOLA'S VOICE (you can nail this)

- She's the eldest daughter and the family general, from Ashvein's Sixth Ward. She handles everything for everybody and has never asked for help.
- Her inner voice is funny, blunt and grown, a little dry. When it's bad, it's deadpan: "Oh. So she was fucked."
- She reads a lie in three sentences.
- When she's attracted to someone, it shows up as irritation first. She clocks that he's fine, gets annoyed about it, and goes back to work. Her comparisons come from her own family (Danika moved to Ironcross for Alexi and never looked back).
- She measures herself against her mama and her daddy, not against abstractions.
- Her dialogue carries the dialect ("I ain't trying to have no debt tied to someone in Shadowmere"). Her narration is mostly standard, with some slide ("til payday came around on Friday").

## SILAS'S VOICE (this is the hard one, so read the Xaden chapters)

Silas is older than Isola and a Black man from Shadowmere. Shadowmere is his home, and he runs it. Write him Xaden-coded, through his own Underside life:

- **His head is loud but never analytical.** It's a running stream of contempt, cussing and self-argument. Write the reaction ("Fuck." / "*Since when, motherfucker?*" / "*Oh, hell no.*"), never the reasoning chain. Don't make him walk through his logic on the page. If he works something out, he says it out loud, short, as a claim: "I'm starting to think it's about to become my family's business too."
- **He judges people in a few words and moves on.** "Ora had the softest voice in Shadowmere and the hardest ledger."
- **His threats are decisions he's holding back.** "He'd let him keep the eyes. Tonight."
- **His humor is menace.** "You got a problem with the difference, take it up with Dante." / "You trying to get me killed?" / "You do good at that all by yourself."
- **His orders are one sentence.** He doesn't brief people. "Anybody asks about me and a girl, you remember their face."
- **He notices a fact and doesn't explain what it means** (Pell's hands shaking, Dante's shoes and keys).
- **With Isola:** he denies wanting her and confesses worse in the same breath. Control is his identity, and she's what knocks it loose. His body reacts before he admits anything. He calls her "Salt."
- **Remy and Camille are his Garrick and Sgaeyl.** They roast him, and he answers with irritation and threats, never anything wistful. Never write "What'd she say?" energy.
- **His city moves around him.** The dice bank lifts two fingers, Booker won't take his money, runners take his coin. Show it through how people act, not narration.
- **Loyalty shows up as names.** Remy, Jude, Rhea, and the fifteen-year-old runner nobody found.
- Model his chapters on my Ch1, Ch3 and Ch7.

## CANON AND WHERE WE ARE

**The world:**
- The Underside is nine tiers: Crownlight, Dawnveil, Ironcross, Stoneveil, Lowtorch, Ashvein, Shadowmere, Gravebend, Nightroot.
- The Synod enforces with Shrouds (veiled, made of name strips, moving under Silence) and Quietcasters.
- Halflight is owned by Ironcross.
- Veilmarks are the currency. Counterfeiting them is High Heresy, punished by public Silencing.
- Hush is a Silencecraft drug that dulls grief.
- Graves family magic is Graveshand and threshold/funeral thread.
- Wraithmoor names answer on the family name wall.
