II.2

MACRO GEOGRAPHY

Place the lands relative to each other without inventing cartography.

The four lands occupy multiple major landmasses and regions in one world.

CANON TCD-025.

Four placements are fixed:
Remnia and Somnoria are separate continental landmasses with ocean between them.
The Underside attaches to the Zenithia side, or corner, of Remnia's broader geography and
descends from there. CANON TCD-025, TCD-026
Thalara, primarily underwater, lies farther outward in the island and sea region.
Relative position is described by a Spain / France / Italy / Greek-islands comparison. That
comparison controls relative placement only.
GUARDRAIL

What the analogy does not mean.
It doesn't import real borders, coastlines, scale, distances, climate, culture, languages, or history.
It doesn't make any land a disguised version of a real country. It doesn't settle which way
anything faces on a map. It tells you roughly where the lands sit in relation to one another, and
nothing more.

The Underside's placement deserves its own sentence because it's the easiest to get wrong. It
isn't a buried continent lying flat beneath Remnia. It's a vertically tiered nation that begins at or
near the surface off Zenithia's side and descends. Its top tiers, Crownlight and Dawnveil, are
above ground; by Ironcross the descent is fully subterranean. CANON TCD-026. Part III builds
the full cross-section.

II.4

KNOWLEDGE OF THE OTHER
LANDS
Kill universal-awareness shortcuts.

There's no universal answer to "do people know about the other lands?" Awareness varies by
nation, class, institution, profession, network, person, and time. CANON TCD-016. A smuggler, a
Sanctum archivist, and a miner's kid in the same land can hold three different pictures, and all
three can change between chapter 5 and chapter 25.
The six levels from I.5 do the sorting: existence, location, functional, route, historical, and true
Tandem knowledge. None implies another. Someone can know Somnoria's name and nothing
about where it is. Someone can use a route without knowing what's on the far side of the
boundary it crosses. Someone can know a land's history as their own institution teaches it,
which may be the least reliable version available.
Four kinds of source feed a character's picture, and the Companion tracks them separately:
what their institution teaches or officially claims, what their network passes around as rumor or
trade knowledge, what's been suppressed or rewritten, and what they've learned firsthand. An
error counts as knowledge for drafting purposes. A character acts on what she believes, and the
Companion records the belief with its lane label.
What the drafted pages already establish. These are seed entries for the full matrix in VII.5.
Every row is dated to its chapter; none is a claim about what the character's whole society
knows.

II.5

Explain shared deep origin without making magics interchangeable.

All four modern magical traditions ultimately draw from one primordial magical source or
substrate that predates the modern division of the Tandem world. CANON TCD-035. The Divide
didn't create four unrelated magics. Geography, environment, bodies, bloodlines, culture,
survival, history, and institutions reshaped one shared power into systems that are now
genuinely distinct, with their own access, costs, classifications, and cultural explanations.
Both halves of that ruling carry weight. The shared root means cross-land resonance is possible
without anyone inventing a coincidence. The genuine distinctness means a Somnorian dream
technique doesn't work in Remnia just because both descend from the same source, and a
Thalaran seal cadence isn't a Silencecraft seal with a different accent. Long isolation after the
Divide accelerated the specialization. CANON TCD-045

II.6

CROSSBLOOD: A LABEL, NOT A
BIOLOGY
Stop one institutional theory from becoming taxonomy.

"Crossblood" is an institutional, scholarly, political, or historical label for people believed to
carry inheritance associated with more than one separated Tandem tradition. It isn't an
objectively correct universal biological category. CANON TCD-036. Institutions may define it
differently through incomplete theory, bloodline politics, fear, or propaganda, and the label
never establishes what a person's magic objectively is.
The older two-world lore treated Crossbloods as a settled pre-fracture biology and offered a
candidate list that grouped Katherine, Cassius, and Zephyr. That list is LEGACY / SUPERSEDED. Its
three names don't share an anomaly: the rulings now explain Katherine and Zephyr in
incompatible ways, and nothing established about Cassius puts him in either of their
categories.
NOT THE SAME AS

Rare, ancient, hybrid, inherited, or anomalous magic is not Crossblood magic. "Crossblood" is a
claim someone makes about a person. Whether it's true is a separate question, answered case by
case.

established" column shows why each person needs individual review instead of a shared label.

Separate author-level text from in-world textual history.

A complete Prophecy of the Shattered Veil exists at the author and universe level.

CANON TCD-

006. No intact, authoritative master copy of it is presently known in-world. CANON TCD-047.
What the four lands have instead are four divergent historical descendants: Remnia's
Breathbound Law, Somnoria's Dreamwalker's Writ, the Underside's Mourning Verse, and
Thalara's Drowned Canticle. They aren't four physical pieces of one document. Nobody can
assemble the prophecy by collecting them.
What characters can do is reconstruct. Comparing the variants against historical, magical,
material, and testimonial evidence can recover wording, underlying truth, or both. No national
version is presumed untouched. CANON TCD-047

II.14

Define one inherited working across cultures.

The Elders' Prayer is one inherited magical working that survives in more than one culture
under different language and ritual expression. CANON TCD-023. Its objective function is
specific: it restores a living person's viable magical and life pattern when that pattern has been
severed, corrupted, or catastrophically dysregulated. CANON TCD-057. Restored magical
continuity can stabilize secondary bodily functions and call someone back from active dying, as
long as a viable living pattern remains.
It has hard edges. It can't resurrect completed death. It can't regenerate destroyed anatomy.

II.16

Place the books in time without chapter lockstep.

The books are placed in time by macro windows, not by chapter lockstep.

CANON TCD-013. 3SL

happens about two years before the main concurrent period. ADST begins when Elias goes
missing, and FTE begins a couple of weeks later. The two then run largely concurrently and end
around the same time, without requiring their chapters to line up one for one.
TSWK and the next Shadowmere installment form the next concurrent window. They begin
directly after FTE and ADST, with no meaningful gap of months or years; a few days or small
weeks for logistics are fine. They overlap each other substantially without needing identical
opening dates.

CANON TCD-014, TCD-024, TCD-054. The handoff is rolling: conditions for the

later plots may already be active during late FTE and ADST, and still-later stories may begin
activating before the prior pair fully resolves.
Four bands per book. Every book is tracked by four separate dates, because "when does it
