IV.3

POWER AND JURISDICTION
Replace "who's in charge" with a real power map.

Remnia doesn't have one ruler. It has several centers of authority that overlap, compete, and
sometimes need each other. CANON TCD-041. The Crown is the highest public civil and
dynastic authority and directly controls London Town and imperial affairs. Provincial Leaders
administer their territories under titles that vary. The Provincial Leadership Board gives the
provinces formal representation, and a Conqueror's one-year seat can change it. The Elemental
Court is a separate political-magical bloc with influence over several provinces and over
elemental policy, but not over London Town. The Sanctum is semi-autonomous in magical
regulation, bloodlines, Tournament law, doctrine, security, and historical control. It isn't simply
the Crown's agency, and it isn't legally omnipotent over the Crown either.
The practical consequence for drafting: when something goes wrong in FTE, the first question
is never "who's in charge?" It's "whose jurisdiction is this, and who else thinks it's theirs?"

Dossier for the institution behind most FTE secrets.

The Sanctum is the institution FTE is actually about. It regulates magic, bloodlines, Tournament
law, doctrine, security, and historical control. CANON TCD-041. It runs the Tournament in public
as a sacred tradition and a political reward, and in private uses it to classify, cultivate, control,
exploit, monitor, suppress, or eliminate dangerous people.

CANON TCD-034. It is the principal

enforcer of Katherine's concealment and custodian of the records.

CANON TCD-059.

and may exploit the London Town forfeit but doesn't cause it (TCD-042, TCD-052), and it didn't
originate Katherine's erasure (TCD-059). An institution that frightening is more frightening
when it's also bounded.

THE TOURNAMENT: STRUCTURE
AND PURPOSE

IV.6

Explain both faces of the Tournament.

The Chess Tournament pits two provinces against each other with living Pieces on a
chessboard's positions. This year it's Old Prosperity against London Town. The Empress's
opening letter sets the public terms: thirty-two Pieces, "sixteen from each province, eight men
and eight women"; the goal is to "capture the opposing King. Achieve Checkmate. Win."; and "to
the victor, I offer a seat on the Provincial Leadership Board — a year of power, a year of favor, a
year to reshape your province's future." (

CURRENT DRAFTED TEXT, front matter)

That's the public half. The private half is canon too: the Sanctum uses selection, assignments,
challenges, stress, injuries, alliances, and survival to classify, cultivate, control, exploit, monitor,
suppress, or eliminate dangerous people.

CANON TCD-034. Neither half cancels the other. The

Board seat is real, and so is the cull.
The Tournament is designed around seven Challenges, and reaching the Seventh isn't
guaranteed. Attrition, Checkmate, disqualification, intervention, or another decisive outcome
can end it early. In the current FTE, the Sanctum declares Challenge Six decisive and
terminates the Tournament.

CANON TCD-007

REVISION FLAG (FTE BETA)

"Six challenges."
The Empress's letter calls the Tournament "survival distilled into six challenges." TCD-007 makes
the design seven, with the Sixth ending this one. The letter's wording needs revising to fit the
ruling. It doesn't compete with it.
