IV.11

REMNIAN LINEAGE: THE
WORKING RECORD

Rebuilt from the evidence, 2026-10-09. A derived record, not a source.

This record rebuilds the Remnian imperial and royal lineage from the sources, claim by claim.
Each line names the source it comes from and carries the status that source earns under the
authority order: rulings, then the Beta, then current author decisions, development, reference,
shared lore, supporting artifacts, and legacy. It isn’t a source itself, and it settles nothing a
ruling leaves open.
Five layers stay apart, as IV.11 requires: biological, legal family status, public and recorded, title,
and succession. A parent never fills a succession slot, and a title never proves a parent. Where
two sources disagree, both stay on the page with their sources, and the disagreement gets a
number (LC-nn) and a working block in lane D.
LOCKED ANCHOR ·

CANON TCD-040

Tobias Lockwood is the biological father of Katherine (with Michelle Nicholas) and of Melena
(with Lucia Hastings-Lockwood). Katherine and Melena are paternal half-sisters.
That doesn’t make Katherine legally acknowledged, a Lockwood by law, a princess, publicly
known, or an heir. TCD-059 governs the concealment only.
GUARDRAIL

Agreement between two weak sources isn’t canon. Two timelines that agree are still
two timelines.
