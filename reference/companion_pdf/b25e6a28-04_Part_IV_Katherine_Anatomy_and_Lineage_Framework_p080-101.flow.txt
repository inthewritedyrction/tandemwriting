CONFLICT

Melena's eyes, now with more evidence.
The Beta gives her "Lockwood amber eyes" and says Cordelia shares them. 3SL gives her green.
FTE 31–39 has Tobias's eyes "the same shade as hers" and green. The Character Bible says
emerald. The Remnian Timeline Before Story Start says green "associated with the Hastings
lineage" and gives Elias golden Lockwood eyes; the unaudited Imperial Lineage repeats it. The
Companion preserves the conflict (III.20). The Beta controls FTE's drafted page; nobody has
ruled which book changes.
REVISION FLAG (DEVELOPMENT CHAPTERS)

The parents' elements.
One Mirror line in 31–39 makes Tobias a Frost and Lucia a Flamer. The Beta has it the other way
round: Lucia was "one of the most powerful" Frosts in the empire, Cordelia inherited her Frost
("Second generation"), and Melena "took after their father — fire running through her veins instead
of ice" (ch. 8). The Beta controls; the Mirror lines need revising. Neither version says which side
Katherine's ancestral capacity comes from (TCD-049). A parent's element isn't evidence of an
inherited anatomy.

Succession as the sources frame it. Welcome to Remnia describes a strictly matrilineal
Hastings succession and a Crown that named Elias heir after Lucia's death to preserve its
image. 31–39 has a Privy Council push Elias into the role. The Remnian Timeline Before Story
Start (repeated in the unaudited Imperial Lineage) has Lucia decree at Melena's birth that
daughters keep their place ahead of younger brothers. None of this is ruled. The one drafted
fact is that Melena is second in line.
GUARDRAIL

Proving Tobias's paternity (IV.13) doesn't make Katherine a princess, a Lockwood, or an heir. Each
of those is a separate decision, and some of them are the Crown's to make in-story.
