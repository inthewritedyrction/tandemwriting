**3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Chapter Synopses

*Silas Graves settles the Underside's debts with his hands. Isola Wraithmoor holds her family together with her name. A counterfeit coin and a hooded girl with fire she can't keep put them in each other's way, and a claim neither of them asked for puts them under one roof at 3 Shadowmere Lane.*

**Part One · Collision**

**Chapters 1 to 9.** *Silas Graves is handed a girl the Synod wants. Isola Wraithmoor finds a coin that could sink her family. Six seconds in a narrow lane puts them in each other's way.*

**1. Twenty-Four**

**SILAS · THE GUTTERLIGHT RING**

Jerrick sold a runner route, and three young runners vanished on it. In the Gutterlight Ring, Silas Graves ends Jerrick on Mother Rae's two-finger signal, his twenty-fourth sanctioned kill, and the salt rite makes it legal. One runner turns up alive. The youngest is still out there somewhere, and by the time Silas gets home to 3C, a writ is waiting: his father wants him at noon.

**2. Thirty Days**

**ISOLA · ASHVEIN**

All Isola Wraithmoor wants is one day in Ashvein where nobody needs her. By breakfast she's talked Tevin out of a fight and bound her younger brother's name to the family wall in her father's cadence, and the name holds. Her mother wakes with a cold headache and a warning she won't explain. Asha's bag, meanwhile, is packed for somewhere, and the coin hidden in its lining is fake.

**3. Not Regular Cargo**

**SILAS · SHADOWMERE**

Dante Graves hands his son a job and keeps the explanation: a hooded girl the Synod is hunting, with fire she can't keep in her hands. Silas only has to get her to a Gutterlight safehouse without anyone looking twice. In the thread-seller lane, a stranger swings a ringed fist at his chest. He catches it an inch from his sternum, and she still won't give him her name.

**4. Pell's Stall**

**ISOLA · THE BACK MARKET**

Isola crosses the chasm bridge on her godmother's token with a fake coin in her pocket and an order for funeral-grade salt. The Back Market assayer takes one look at the coin and won't weigh it. At Pell's stall, two silent men collect an envelope and stare too long at the token in her hand. She finally spots Asha in the crowd, and a stranger shielding a hooded girl backs right into her.

**5. No Windows**

**SILAS · GUTTERLIGHT**

The girl Silas is guarding has one rule: nobody touches her without asking. She burns through the safehouse lock and runs, and he catches up to her in an alley just as Shrouds pass under Silence. Hiding her means breaking her rule. Back inside, Camille warns him the ward won't hold a week, and while Melena opens her mother's letter, Silas sees a name in it that isn't hers.

**6. Name Clears**

**ISOLA · SIXTH WARD, ASHVEIN**

Asha comes home late with quicksilver on her cuff, and when Isola checks her cousin's strip on the family name wall, the funeral thread goes slack. A Wraithmoor name only does that when someone is leaving it. The route strip hidden in Asha's boot explains why, right down to the next drop. Micah, meanwhile, hears Isola describe the man from the lane and laughs. He knows exactly whose brother that is.

**7. Remy**

**SILAS · THE GUTTERLIGHT SAFEHOUSE**

Remy shows up at first bell with breakfast. When Melena's fire flares over her mother's letter, he talks her down with a silver wrapper and a quiet voice, and Silas watches his friend do in minutes what he couldn't manage all night. She almost shows them a move her father taught her and stops herself. By the time she eats, they have a plan, and it runs through a Blue Circle den and something called Hush.

**8. Blue Circle**

**SILAS · MISS ORA'S DEN**

Remy walks them into Miss Ora's den through a door that isn't on any Mercer map, and Silas files that away for later. The plan is one measured drop of Hush, enough to see whether it quiets Melena's fire. Melena pays for a full vial with a mourning stone instead. By the end of the night, Blue Circle is in pieces, and the only threshold left that can hold her is Silas's own.

**9. Final Bell**

**ISOLA · BLUE CIRCLE**

Isola tails Asha to Blue Circle and watches the man from the lane walk in with a red-haired girl right before the den blows. Hiding from the runners afterward, she hears enough to know her cousin is in deep: the burned package is being charged to somebody, and someone called Blackwood is collecting. From the rail bridge, she watches Silas get a man's arm ready to break. He knows she's there.

**Part Two · The Climb**

**Chapters 10 to 20.** *A private route turns into the only one she has left, and answering it costs Silas every law his father ever taught him.*

**10. Blue Six**

**SILAS · SHADOWMERE**

Silas gets a location out of a burned runner without breaking anything: Blue Six. His father answers by suspending his access, and when Silas sets the sold mourning stone on Dante's desk and asks who the girl really is, Dante won't say. Jude ties the counterfeit to the token from Jerrick's locker. Mother Rae sets a meeting at Halflight with Micah's sister, and Dante tells Silas to keep his hands off her. Silas asks which way he means that.

**11. Halflight**

**ISOLA · HALFLIGHT**

At one small table in Halflight, their knees almost touch, and the counterfeit coin passes between them more often than it needs to. Isola came to call in a debt. Silas came for answers, and the first time he reaches for the coin, she closes her fist and makes him ask. He gives her a private route called black three. Before she leaves, she gives him something she refused him in the lane, or at least half of it.

**12. Elias**

**SILAS · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Melena has the second floor of 3 Shadowmere Lane now, one floor under Silas, with a knocking radiator between them. One glass of blackroot doesn't set anything on fire. Instead, it loosens her mouth, and she reads her mother's last letter out loud, a letter addressed to somebody named Elias. Before the night's over, Silas finally learns who he's been guarding. Remy stays, because she asked him to.

**13. Audit**

**ISOLA · SIXTH WARD, ASHVEIN**

The night before the audit, Isola sends black three a message that has nothing to do with the coin: “You make it home?” Silas answers in under a minute, and she falls asleep with the slate in her hand. By morning, Shrouds and Synod inspectors are tearing through Sixth Ward. When the inspector reads Asha's name out loud, Isola feels it start to slip, and she has to hold it under the table without anyone noticing.

**14. Tell Me You Good**

**SILAS · SHADOWMERE**

In the sparring ring, Remy admits he likes Melena and lets slip a name Silas has never heard before. Silas has his own secret, and it lives on his slate: a thread of messages he's reread more times than he'd ever admit. Remy notices anyway. When the route board goes black and Halflight's gate turns red, Silas types four words to the one person in Ashvein who isn't answering.

**15. Open Halflight**

**ISOLA · SIXTH WARD, ASHVEIN**

Asha escapes through the vent mid-fight, and the order in locker 114 makes the whole Wraithmoor household pay if she fails. When a Shroud comes for her cousin carrying a name strip that's going black, Isola rips it out of its hands and holds the name herself. Now the Shroud wants her instead. With Halflight's gate locked, the only route she has left is the one Silas gave her.

**16. Nineteen Minutes**

**SILAS · HALFLIGHT**

For nineteen minutes, Silas does exactly what his father told him. He stays on his side of Halflight's bars and watches a Shroud close in on Isola while she holds onto someone's name with everything she has. The Shroud carries a Graves mark on its forehead, cut with a hook he'd know anywhere. Minute twenty belongs to him.

**17. Funeral Road**

**SILAS · THE FUNERAL ROAD**

Silas carries Isola home through the funeral road, and the only person who can guide him is the sister who hasn't spoken to him in a year. Rhea answers anyway, through blood. Halfway up the service stair, Isola wakes long enough to tell him he's holding her wrong, and he fixes it exactly the way she says. Rhea is waiting at the door. She takes one look at how he's carrying Isola and doesn't say a word.

**18. Funeral Terms**

**SILAS · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Rhea sings the Underchant over Isola's leg, and the deathwork in it doesn't budge. The funeral terms have to go somewhere. They follow Graveshand back up into Silas, and every lamp in 3 Shadowmere Lane goes dark at once. In the black, the dead start calling him by all his names.

**19. Mine**

**SILAS · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

The dead are flooding him, and something wearing his father's shoulders is striking the door. Rhea and Remy both tell him not to take the only way left to keep Isola alive. Silas knows exactly what it will cost her. She's too far gone to hear him say he's sorry. He says it anyway, and he does it anyway.

**20. Her Name on His Wrist**

**ISOLA · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Isola wakes up on a table with her own name written on Silas Graves's wrist. Distance hurts them both, and the worst part is how her body calms down when he's close. She tries to take her name back the way her father taught her, and it answers Silas instead. Downstairs, Melena hears her mother's voice in the fire, and the letter meant for Elias is about to go with it.

**Part Three · The Address**

**Chapters 21 to 31.** *One apartment, one bed, a line of pillows down the middle, and a claim only her own name can loosen.*

**21. The Burial Rail**

**SILAS · THE BURIAL RAIL**

Camille can't close what Silas opened, and Isola wants her mama. The fastest way to Sixth Ward is the burial rail under his building, so they ride it tethered, with Melena refusing to be left behind. Somewhere in the dark, Isola moves his palm higher against her ribs and holds it there. Neither of them looks at the other. Silas tells himself exactly what that touch is for and keeps his hand where she put it.

**22. Omari's House**

**SILAS · SIXTH WARD, ASHVEIN**

Sixth Ward is under audit, and Omari Wraithmoor has just met the man whose wrist carries his daughter's name. Silas lets him swing. When he finally gets Omari's arm in the same hold Isola watched on the rail bridge, he looks at her before he decides what to do with it. Selah begins the Prayer at her kitchen table, and partway through, she says a name Silas never expected to hear in Ashvein.

**23. Lucia**

**ISOLA · SIXTH WARD, ASHVEIN**

Selah keeps Silas breathing with frost climbing past her elbows, and Isola makes sure he knows where they stand: she wants his claim off her, and saving her life doesn't make it okay. Asha comes home with news from the Synod. They have the fire girl's real name now. Remy takes Melena's hand and gives her a new one, and Selah, hands still frozen, tells her something about her mother that no Remnian record holds.

**24. The Station**

**SILAS · THE STATION**

Isola could stay home. She chooses 3 Shadowmere Lane instead, because she won't bring Silas's dead inside her mother's walls. Getting there means learning to walk as one person, and every route back is dead. Remy opens a door that shouldn't exist into a station nobody's supposed to know about, with a mark on his wrist he's never explained. When the gold audit sweeps through, Isola pulls Silas into a recess built for one.

**25. Her Terms**

**ISOLA · 3C**

Isola's first day living tethered in 3C comes with rules, and she writes every one of them. Silas adds one of his own, and it's the word she gave him at Halflight. The apartment has one bed. He takes the floor without being asked, and she lasts two nights before she builds a wall of pillows down the middle and tells him the floor is stupid.

**26. Dante at the Door**

**SILAS · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Camille brings Jude home, and Dante Graves follows them up the stairs. For the first time, he gets a real look at Isola and at what his son did to keep her alive, and Silas can see him working out what that's worth. Rhea and her father share a room for the first time in a year. Silas asks about the hook mark on the Shroud. Dante admits the hook is his. How it got onto a Shroud, he won't say here.

**27. Bait**

**ISOLA · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Isola runs the counterplan from Silas's kitchen table: deliver the twelve-coin order into Blue Six as bait and let Danika's watch see who picks it up. The tether puts Silas at every meeting, and they turn out to be good at this together. Asha catches her smiling at him over the map. Isola says she's tied to him. Asha points out that isn't what she asked.

**28. Mira**

**SILAS · THE BACK MARKET**

Remy spends a night teaching Melena how to be somebody else in the Back Market: how to read a lantern, and how to answer to Mira. Silas trails behind and watches his best friend fall in love. The warehouse crew is asking around for a red-haired girl. When Tiran Blackwood finds her face to face, he has a debt with her name on it, and for half a second, he and Remy look like they know each other.

**29. Say It**

**ISOLA · 3C**

Rhea explains what Mine means under Graves law, and Isola makes Silas say it to her face anyway. He says every word, including the part where he'd do it again. She tells him exactly what it took from her. At the end of it, he hands her Camille's old name-rite ledgers, everything she'd need to never need him again. She's close enough to kiss him when she stops, for a reason he can't argue with.

**30. Who Collects**

**SILAS · THE FUNERAL-SHOP ROOF**

The bait goes into Blue Six, and Silas and Isola watch it from the funeral-shop roof for hours in the cold. She slips her hands into his coat pocket and asks afterward if that's okay. He says yes too fast. The crew that comes to collect has a kid running for them, and Silas has been looking for that kid since the night he killed Jerrick.

**31. Her Own Name**

**ISOLA · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Isola has the rites now, and three women who know name-work standing around her. She speaks her full name over the letters on Silas's wrist, knowing exactly what Mine means, and decides how much of it to keep. The rite costs her something. Afterward she walks to the far end of 3 Shadowmere Lane to test it, and she stands there long enough to be sure.

**Part Four · What They Choose**

**Chapters 32 to 42 and the Epilogue.** *The tether can't make anyone stay now. Getting Melena home means one block of the worst ground in Shadowmere, and the whole Address walks her to it.*

**32. Apart**

**SILAS · 3C**

The morning after the tether loosens, Isola goes home to Sixth Ward, and Silas walks her to the burial rail and stays on the platform. Distance aches, and his heart keeps beating anyway. Alone in 3C, he notices every place she's been, and the dead on his chain go quiet without her. Remy has found where the corridor comes out and won't pretend he likes it. Across the street, Dante's people are watching the building.

**33. She Knocks**

**ISOLA · 3C**

Isola finally gets a night at home with her family: Micah's sponsorship restored and Asha swearing she's done buying names. Her own bed won't let her sleep. She rides the burial rail back across the city and knocks on 3C instead of using her key, and when Silas opens the door, she asks first. This time, the yes is his to give.

**34. Interest**

**SILAS · THE BACK MARKET**

Tiran Blackwood starts collecting interest on the fire girl's debt, and something the Address can't replace goes up in flames with nobody able to prove who lit it. Only Silas notices what Tiran left untouched: everything that belongs to Remy. The Synod puts a price on Melena's real name, and every stranger in Shadowmere becomes a risk. When Silas finally sees the man who runs warehouse row, he does something new. He takes it to Isola first.

**35. Where the Door Goes**

**ISOLA · WAREHOUSE ROW**

The corridor's far door opens under warehouse row, right in the middle of the crew's ground, and past it is the way up to Remnia. Getting Melena home means crossing one block of the worst street in Shadowmere. Isola is the only one who can walk it unseen, crutch and all, so she maps every step. Mother Rae promises to cover the block, and she means it. Before morning, Nightmare calls her across the border into Somnoria, and nobody tells Nightmare no.

**36. The Last Good Night**

**SILAS · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Remy cooks for everyone at the Address the night before the crossing, and for a few hours it feels like a family. He dances Melena around the kitchen. Silas and Isola wash dishes side by side, and under the counter where nobody can see, she takes his hand. Later, on the roof, Remy asks Silas for one promise if tomorrow goes wrong, and Silas gives it to him.

**37. Mira**

**MELENA · WAREHOUSE ROW**

Isola walks Melena to the station door, and Silas holds it open, just like he promised. Remy takes her hand. The corridor comes out under warehouse row in the pouring rain, one block from the way home. Halfway down the alley, the crew is waiting. When Melena looks back, the door they came through is already shut.

**38. The Fire**

**SILAS · WAREHOUSE ROW**

Silas closed the station door to save Rhea and Isola from the audit, and now he has to reach warehouse row the long way. By the time he gets there, it's burning. Melena's fire isn't death, so Graveshand can't end it. He walks in anyway and comes out carrying her, and he won't leave Remy to the fire. Across the city, through the loosened tether, Isola feels him stop breathing.

**39. This Is On You**

**ISOLA · WAREHOUSE ROW**

Isola leaves Silas sealed in Rhea's rite and goes after Melena. Tiran Blackwood finds her first, behind the row in the rain, screaming at the only person left to blame for Remy. Isola gets there on Veil-Slip, too late to stop it. By morning, Tiran has cleaned up the scene, and the Synod's official story already blames the fire girl. Mother Rae arrives late, with dream essence still on her hands.

**40. Graves Rites**

**SILAS · 3 SHADOWMERE LANE**

Remy's funeral is held at 3 Shadowmere Lane. Silas, out of Rhea's coffin, burned and still breathing smoke, closes his best friend's eyes while Rhea sings the Underchant. When he breaks, Isola holds him, and nothing is making her stay. Dante stands at the back of the room. Melena remembers one line from the letter that burned, and nobody there knows who it's talking about.

**41. Mira Leaves**

**ISOLA · THE REMNIAN CROSSING**

Isola and Silas walk Melena back through the station to the Remnian crossing, with Rhea leading the way. She's keeping her promise to Remy: down here, she was only ever Mira. The friendship that started on a landing at 3 Shadowmere Lane gets its goodbye, and Isola sends her up with something to keep. Up top, Remnia is waiting for Melena Lockwood, and Mira stays down here.

**42. You Make It Home?**

**SILAS · THE BURIAL RAIL**

Isola goes home to the Wraithmoor house, because her family needs her and her leg needs her mother. Silas walks her to the burial rail, and this time, in front of everyone on the platform, she kisses him first. Back in 3C, the second floor below him is empty and her name is still on his wrist. Her ring is sitting on his table. Late that night, black three lights up.

**Epilogue**

**ISOLA · 3C · WINTER 2096**

Some time later, black three is still how they talk, and Isola knocks on 3C again. This time she's asking to be claimed on purpose, in her own voice, with his hand exactly where she puts it. A message arrives folded in a silver wrapper, and they both know who sent it before they read a word. Over in Sixth Ward, the lights in Mother Rae's shop come back on.
